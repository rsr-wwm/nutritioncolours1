Carbohydrate Partitioning and Postprandial Glycemia

Isocaloric metabolic ward studies indicate that identical carbohydrate challenges elicit substantially lower glycemic and insulinemic excursions when consumed in the early morning compared to the late evening.

Consuming complex carbohydrates earlier in the diurnal cycle aligns with higher insulin sensitivity and enhanced postprandial glucose clearance.

Verified Clinical Finding Human Trial

Early time-restricted feeding improves 24-hour mean blood glucose and attenuates glycemic spikes without requiring intentional caloric deficits.

PubMed: 24467926 →

Nocturnal Melatonin and Beta-Cell Inhibition

Late-evening nutrient ingestion coincides with the biological night, marked by rising endogenous melatonin secretion. Melatonin directly inhibits pancreatic beta-cell insulin secretion through high-affinity MTNR1B receptors MTNR1B receptors Melatonin Receptor 1B, an inhibitory G-protein coupled receptor in pancreatic islets that blunts insulin exocytosis. Physiological Role: Pancreatic nocturnal glucose regulation Ref: PMID: 26976620 View in Glossary → .

Elevated circulating melatonin during late evening hours suppresses pancreatic beta-cell insulin secretion, resulting in relative nocturnal glucose intolerance when high-glycemic meals are ingested close to sleep onset.

Verified Clinical Finding Mechanistic RCT

Concomitant elevation of circulating melatonin and glucose induces acute beta-cell fatigue and delays glucose clearance kinetics.
