No physical clinics or city branches

NutritionColours is strictly an online-only platform. We do not operate customer-facing physical offices, walk-in clinics, diagnostic centres, or geographic branch networks. All legacy locality drafts have been permanently retracted under HTTP 410 Gone tombstones.

Important notice before contacting us

  • Do not send confidential medical records, diagnostic laboratory reports, or urgent acute health details through email or messaging apps.
  • This website does not provide acute emergency services, prescription pharmacology, or individual diagnostic evaluation.
  • For individualized health decisions or urgent concerns, continue working directly with an appropriately qualified local medical practitioner.
