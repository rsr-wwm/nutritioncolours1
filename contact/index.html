Medical Disclaimer & Practice Scope

NutritionColours provides evidence-graded clinical nutrition and chrononutrition consulting. Our programs are designed to support metabolic health and complement your physician's medical care. We do not prescribe pharmaceuticals or provide emergency medical services. Always consult your primary physician before altering any prescription medications.

Intake Coordination & Practice Guidelines

Contact our support team to register for clinical metabolic programs, coordinate digital intake records, or query existing wellness plans.

New Patient Intake Protocol

To maintain clinical safety and precision, all prospective clients must complete our initial intake review. During this process, you will submit a detailed medical history, current medication list, and recent blood diagnostic panels. This data allows our clinical nutritionists to design a personalized circadian feeding window and select specific botanical swaps tailored to your unique metabolic profile.

Initial reviews are scheduled within 48 business hours of intake form submission. We coordinate with you via secure email and WhatsApp to finalize onboarding details.

Telehealth Consultation Guidelines

All coaching sessions and consulting reviews are conducted entirely online. Secure video consultations are scheduled to fit your local time zone. Our practitioners review daily digital food logs, answer chrononutrition queries, and adjust swap strategies in real-time.

For urgent dietary adjustments, enrolled clients have direct access to our messaging support desk, ensuring continuous guidance throughout your metabolic program.

Diagnostic Lab Coordination & Safety

Our remote practice operates under strict safety protocols. We do not prescribe pharmaceuticals or alter medical plans. Clients are directed to obtain baseline and follow-up biomarker panels (HbA1c, fasting insulin, full lipid profiles, thyroid panels, and organ clearance markers) from certified local laboratories in their home cities.

Our team reviews these external lab reports to monitor metabolic trends and optimize dietary strategies, working in full alignment with your primary care physician's standard treatments to prioritize your health.

All client files, progress logs, and communication data are protected under strict security guidelines to ensure patient confidentiality and data privacy.
