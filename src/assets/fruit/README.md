# NutritionColours editorial fruit image masters

These three PNGs were generated on 23 September 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool for the public JPGs of the same fruit names. Each is a text-free square still life on a warm pale stone surface with soft daylight and a natural matte look. The apple image served as a **style reference only** for banana and orange generation. No stock image service was used.

Prompt set: one whole red apple with a short stem and small leaf; one whole ripe curved yellow banana with natural speckles; one whole orange with natural dimpled peel. All prompts specified a single recognizable fruit, generous clean negative space, a soft contact shadow, warm stone backdrop, soft upper-left daylight, and no text, labels, nutrition claims, packaging, people, medical symbols, or watermarks.

The source PNGs are retained here. Public outputs are 1024×1024 JPEGs at `public/images/{apple,banana,orange}_illustration.jpg`. The previous public JPGs are preserved unchanged under `docs/audit/legacy-assets/`.
