## Overview

Cardiac surgery encompasses procedures such as coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG) to reroute blood flow around blocked arteries, valve repair or replacement for damaged heart valves, and aortic surgery for aneurysms or dissections. These are major operations performed by cardiothoracic surgeons, with recovery and follow-up managed by a surgical and cardiology team.

## Nutrition for Post-Surgical Recovery

While the surgery itself is outside nutrition's scope, recovery afterward has genuine dietary relevance: adequate protein intake supports wound and sternal healing; if fluid retention occurs, sodium moderation is often recommended; and patients started on warfarin after mechanical valve replacement need consistent (not necessarily low) vitamin K intake from foods like leafy greens, since fluctuating intake — not the food itself — interferes with dosing. Any post-surgical diet plan should be individualized with your surgical team or a registered dietitian.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This information is educational and is intended to support, not replace, care from your cardiologist or physician. Blood pressure and cholesterol targets, medication decisions, and treatment plans must be individualized by your care team. Dietary changes work alongside — not instead of — prescribed cardiovascular medication and monitoring.
