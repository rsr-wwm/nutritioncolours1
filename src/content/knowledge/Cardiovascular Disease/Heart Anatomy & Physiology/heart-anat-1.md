## Overview

The heart's four chambers, four valves, coronary arteries (which supply the heart muscle itself), and electrical conduction system work together to pump blood throughout the body. Coronary circulation specifically refers to the network of arteries feeding the heart muscle — the same vessels affected by coronary artery disease. This is foundational anatomy and physiology, not a condition treated with diet.

## A Note on Scope

Diet does not alter cardiac structure. Its relevance appears when this anatomy is affected by disease — most notably when coronary circulation narrows due to atherosclerosis. See our coronary artery disease page for evidence-based nutrition guidance relevant to that condition.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This information is educational and is intended to support, not replace, care from your cardiologist or physician. Blood pressure and cholesterol targets, medication decisions, and treatment plans must be individualized by your care team. Dietary changes work alongside — not instead of — prescribed cardiovascular medication and monitoring.
