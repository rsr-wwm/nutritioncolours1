# PCOD / PCOS (Metabolic & Hormonal Reversal)

![PCOD / PCOS (Metabolic & Hormonal Reversal)](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1544367567-0f2fcb009e0b?q=80&w=800)

## Root Cause

Hyperinsulinemia driving ovarian theca cells to overproduce androgens (testosterone), resulting in follicular arrest, anovulation, and metabolic cyst formation.

## Biomarkers

- LH to FSH Ratio
- Free & Total Testosterone
- Fasting Insulin (HOMA-IR)
- DHEAS
- Sex Hormone-Binding Globulin (SHBG)

## Therapeutic Spices

- Ceylon Cinnamon
- Fenugreek
- Holy Basil

## Prohibited Foods

- Commercial A1 Dairy (contains growth factors)
- Refined Sugar
- Soy Isolates
- Ultra-processed seed oils

## Circadian Advice

Implement a protein-rich breakfast within 1 hour of waking to stabilize cortisol and prevent mid-day insulin spikes. Avoid skipping breakfast as it worsens luteinizing hormone (LH) dysregulation.
