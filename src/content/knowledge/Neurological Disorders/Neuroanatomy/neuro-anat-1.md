## Overview

The brain's major structures include the four cerebral lobes (frontal, parietal, temporal, occipital), the basal ganglia (a group of deep structures involved in movement control), the limbic system (hippocampus, amygdala, and cingulate cortex, involved in memory and emotion), the brainstem (midbrain, pons, medulla, controlling vital functions like breathing and heart rate), the cerebellum (coordination and balance), and the ventricular system that produces and circulates cerebrospinal fluid.
