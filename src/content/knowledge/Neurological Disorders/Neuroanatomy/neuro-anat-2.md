## Overview

The spinal cord is organized into cervical, thoracic, lumbar, and sacral segments, carrying ascending sensory tracts (dorsal column, spinothalamic, spinocerebellar) and descending motor tracts (corticospinal, rubrospinal, vestibulospinal). Reflex arcs mediate rapid, involuntary responses, and dermatomes and myotomes map specific skin areas and muscle groups to their corresponding spinal nerve roots — a mapping clinicians use to localize nerve or spinal cord lesions.
