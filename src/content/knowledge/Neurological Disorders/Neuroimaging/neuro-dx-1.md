## Overview

Non-contrast CT is the first-line imaging test in suspected stroke, used to quickly rule out hemorrhage before clot-dissolving medication can be given, and to detect early ischemic changes. CT angiography (CTA) evaluates blood vessels for blockages, while CT perfusion (CTP) assesses blood flow to identify salvageable brain tissue. CT is also used to detect mass effect (tissue shifting from swelling or a mass), hydrocephalus, and skull fractures.
