## Overview

Brain MRI uses different sequences to highlight different tissue characteristics: standard T1, T2, and FLAIR sequences for general anatomy and lesion detection; diffusion-weighted imaging (DWI) to detect acute stroke within minutes of onset; perfusion MRI to assess blood flow; MR angiography and venography (MRA/MRV) to evaluate blood vessels; functional MRI (fMRI) to map brain activity; and specialized techniques like diffusion tensor imaging (DTI) and MR spectroscopy (MRS) used mainly in research and specialized clinical settings.

This is a diagnostic-imaging topic and outside the scope of a nutrition review — MRI findings guide diagnosis and treatment decisions and have no nutritional component.
