## Overview

Neurons communicate through electrical and chemical signaling: a resting membrane potential is maintained by ion gradients, and an action potential fires when the membrane depolarizes past a threshold, propagating along the axon — accelerated in myelinated fibers through saltatory conduction. At synapses, this electrical signal triggers the release of neurotransmitters (glutamate, GABA, dopamine, serotonin, acetylcholine, norepinephrine, among others), which bind to receptors on the next neuron to either excite or inhibit its activity.
