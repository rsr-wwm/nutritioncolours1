## Overview

*Also known as: ascorbic acid.*

Vitamin C is a water-soluble antioxidant and a cofactor for collagen synthesis (skin, blood vessels, wound healing), carnitine and neurotransmitter production, and it greatly enhances absorption of non-heme (plant) iron.

## Recommended intake

| Group | Amount per day |
| :--- | ---: |
| Men 19+ | 90 mg |
| Women 19+ | 75 mg |
| Smokers | +35 mg above RDA |
| Pregnancy | 85 mg |

**Tolerable Upper Intake Level (UL):** 2,000 mg/day for adults; above this, diarrhoea and gastrointestinal upset are common, and there may be a small increase in kidney-stone risk in predisposed people.

## Food sources

Citrus fruits, kiwi, strawberries, guava, amla, bell peppers, broccoli, and Brussels sprouts. It is heat-sensitive, so raw or lightly cooked sources retain the most.

## Deficiency

Severe deficiency causes scurvy — bleeding gums, poor wound healing, bruising, and fatigue.

## Too much

Very high supplemental doses cause GI upset; dietary intakes are safe.
