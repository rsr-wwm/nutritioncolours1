## Overview

*Also known as: biotin.*

Biotin is a coenzyme for carboxylase enzymes involved in fatty-acid synthesis, glucose production, and amino-acid metabolism.

## Recommended intake

| Group | Amount per day |
| :--- | ---: |
| Adults 19+ | 30 mcg (AI) |
| Pregnancy | 30 mcg (AI) |
| Lactation | 35 mcg (AI) |

**Tolerable Upper Intake Level (UL):** No Tolerable Upper Intake Level established. (Values are Adequate Intakes, AI.)

## Food sources

Eggs (cooked — raw egg white blocks absorption), liver, nuts, seeds, salmon, and some vegetables.

## Deficiency

Deficiency is rare and causes hair thinning, a scaly red rash, and neurological symptoms.

## Too much

No known toxicity, but **high-dose biotin supplements can seriously distort common lab tests** (including thyroid and troponin/heart tests) — tell your clinician if you take them.
