## Overview

*Also known as: folate / folic acid.*

Folate is essential for DNA synthesis and cell division, and for forming red blood cells. Adequate folate around conception dramatically reduces the risk of neural-tube birth defects.

## Recommended intake

| Group | Amount per day |
| :--- | ---: |
| Adults 19+ | 400 mcg DFE |
| Pregnancy | 600 mcg DFE |
| Lactation | 500 mcg DFE |

**Tolerable Upper Intake Level (UL):** 1,000 mcg/day of **folic acid** (the synthetic form from supplements and fortified foods) for adults; there is no UL for naturally occurring folate in food.

## Food sources

Dark leafy greens, legumes (lentils, chickpeas), asparagus, avocado, liver, and fortified grains.

## Deficiency

Deficiency causes megaloblastic anaemia and, in early pregnancy, neural-tube defects such as spina bifida.

## Too much

Excess folic acid can mask the anaemia of vitamin B12 deficiency, delaying its diagnosis — hence the UL.
