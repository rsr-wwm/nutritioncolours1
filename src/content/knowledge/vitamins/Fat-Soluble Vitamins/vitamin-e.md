## Overview

*Also known as: alpha-tocopherol.*

Vitamin E is a fat-soluble antioxidant that protects cell membranes from oxidative damage and supports immune function and blood-vessel health.

## Recommended intake

| Group | Amount per day |
| :--- | ---: |
| Adults 19+ | 15 mg |
| Pregnancy | 15 mg |
| Lactation | 19 mg |

**Tolerable Upper Intake Level (UL):** 1,000 mg/day of supplemental alpha-tocopherol for adults, based on the risk of haemorrhage.

## Food sources

Vegetable oils (wheat germ, sunflower, safflower), nuts (almonds, hazelnuts), seeds (sunflower), and green leafy vegetables.

## Deficiency

Dietary deficiency is rare; it mainly occurs in fat-malabsorption disorders and causes nerve damage (peripheral neuropathy) and red-cell fragility.

## Too much

High-dose supplements can impair blood clotting and increase bleeding risk, especially alongside anticoagulant medication.
