# Caffeine Toxicity

![Caffeine Toxicity](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Overdosing energy drinks.

## Solution

Hydration, electrolytes.

## Natural Approach

- Hydration, electrolytes.

## Scientific Name

Caffeine Overdose

## Cause

Overdosing energy drinks.

## Hidden Fact

Caffeine has a half-life of 5 hours; noon coffee affects sleep at night.

## When Age

Teens-30s

## Herbs

L-Theanine (calm jitters).

## Lifestyle

Limit to <400mg caffeine daily.
