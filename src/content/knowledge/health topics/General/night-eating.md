# Night Eating

![Night Eating](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Skipping breakfast/undereating.

## Solution

Regular, high-protein meals throughout day.

## Natural Approach

- Regular, high-protein meals throughout day.

## Scientific Name

Night Eating Syndrome

## Cause

Skipping breakfast/undereating.

## Hidden Fact

Re-setting hunger and satiety hormones reverses this pattern.

## When Age

20s+

## Herbs

Magnesium, 5-HTP.

## Lifestyle

Reset hunger circadian rhythm; sleep.
