# Osteopenia

![Osteopenia](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Sedentary life/low calcium.

## Solution

Calcium-rich foods, Vitamin D.

## Natural Approach

- Calcium-rich foods, Vitamin D.

## Scientific Name

Pre-Osteoporosis

## Cause

Sedentary life/low calcium.

## Hidden Fact

The precursor to osteoporosis; reversible with weights.

## When Age

40s+

## Herbs

Vit K2, Magnesium, Strontium.

## Lifestyle

Weight-bearing exercise; quit smoking.
