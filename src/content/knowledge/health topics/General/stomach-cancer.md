# Stomach Cancer

![Stomach Cancer](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

## Solution

## Natural Approach

## Scientific Name

Gastric Carcinoma

## Cause

High salt/smoked meats feed H. pylori.

## Hidden Fact

Rates are highest in Japan/Korea due to salted foods.

## When Age

50s+

## Herbs

Green Tea extract, Curcumin, Garlic.

## Lifestyle

Treat H. pylori; quit smoking.
