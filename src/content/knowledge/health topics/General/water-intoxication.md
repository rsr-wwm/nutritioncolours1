# Water Intoxication

![Water Intoxication](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Drinking gallons of water without salt.

## Solution

Electrolyte-rich fluids, salty foods.

## Natural Approach

- Electrolyte-rich fluids, salty foods.

## Scientific Name

Hyponatremia

## Cause

Drinking gallons of water without salt.

## Hidden Fact

Drinking too much plain water can cause fatal brain swelling.

## When Age

Athletes

## Herbs

Sodium, Potassium tabs.

## Lifestyle

Drink to thirst during endurance events.
