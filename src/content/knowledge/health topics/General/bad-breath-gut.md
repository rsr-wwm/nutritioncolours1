## Problem

## Solution

## Natural Approach

## Scientific Name

Helicobacter pylori Infection

## Cause

H. pylori from contaminated food.

## Hidden Fact

90% of bad breath originates from the tongue or gut, not teeth.

## When Age

30s+

## Herbs

Matula tea, Mastic gum, Probiotics.

## Lifestyle

Treat H. pylori; brush tongue.
