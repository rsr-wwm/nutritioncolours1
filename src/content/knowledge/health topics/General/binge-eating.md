# Binge Eating

![Binge Eating](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Chronic dieting/starvation.

## Solution

Regular balanced meals (no skipping).

## Natural Approach

- Regular balanced meals (no skipping).

## Scientific Name

Binge Eating Disorder (BED)

## Cause

Chronic dieting/starvation.

## Hidden Fact

Blood sugar crashes trigger the primal brain to binge.

## When Age

20s-40s

## Herbs

5-HTP, Inositol, Magnesium.

## Lifestyle

CBT therapy; blood sugar stability.
