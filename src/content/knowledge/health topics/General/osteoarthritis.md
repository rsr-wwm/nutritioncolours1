# Osteoarthritis

![Osteoarthritis](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Obesity physically crushes cartilage.

## Solution

Anti-inflammatory diet, weight loss.

## Natural Approach

- Anti-inflammatory diet, weight loss.

## Scientific Name

Degenerative Joint Disease

## Cause

Obesity physically crushes cartilage.

## Hidden Fact

Cartilage has no blood vessels, so it heals very poorly.

## When Age

50s+

## Herbs

Glucosamine, Chondroitin, Turmeric.

## Lifestyle

Low-impact cardio (swimming); strength training.
