# Alcoholic Liver Disease

![Alcoholic Liver Disease](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Chronic alcohol poisons liver cells.

## Solution

High protein, high B-vitamins, low fat.

## Natural Approach

- High protein, high B-vitamins, low fat.

## Scientific Name

Alcohol-Related Liver Disease

## Cause

Chronic alcohol poisons liver cells.

## Hidden Fact

The liver can regenerate, but cirrhosis is irreversible scarring.

## When Age

40s+

## Herbs

Milk Thistle, NAC, Phosphatidylcholine.

## Lifestyle

Absolute alcohol cessation.
