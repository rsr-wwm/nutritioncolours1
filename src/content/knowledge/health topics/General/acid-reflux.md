# Acid Reflux

![Acid Reflux](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Overeating/late meals weaken valve.

## Solution

Smaller meals; avoid spicy/fatty.

## Natural Approach

- Smaller meals; avoid spicy/fatty.

## Scientific Name

GERD / GORD

## Cause

Overeating/late meals weaken valve.

## Hidden Fact

Chronic untreated reflux can permanently damage vocal cords.

## When Age

30s+

## Herbs

DGL Licorice, Aloe Vera, Slippery Elm.

## Lifestyle

Don't lie down 3 hrs after eating.
