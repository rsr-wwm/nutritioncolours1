# Esophageal Cancer

![Esophageal Cancer](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Chronic acid reflux burns cells.

## Solution

Weight loss, small meals, low acid.

## Natural Approach

- Weight loss, small meals, low acid.

## Scientific Name

Esophageal Carcinoma

## Cause

Chronic acid reflux burns cells.

## Hidden Fact

Acid reflux changing cells (Barrett's) is a major hidden cause.

## When Age

50s+

## Herbs

DGL Licorice, Zinc Carnosine.

## Lifestyle

Don't lie down after eating; stop smoking.
