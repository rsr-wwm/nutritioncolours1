# Wernicke-Korsakoff

![Wernicke-Korsakoff](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Alcohol stops B1 absorption.

## Solution

Thiamine-rich foods (pork, sunflower seeds).

## Natural Approach

- Thiamine-rich foods (pork, sunflower seeds).

## Scientific Name

Alcohol-Induced Brain Damage

## Cause

Alcohol stops B1 absorption.

## Hidden Fact

Often misdiagnosed as dementia; completely reversible with B1.

## When Age

40s+

## Herbs

High-dose IV/Oral Thiamine (B1).

## Lifestyle

Absolute alcohol cessation.
