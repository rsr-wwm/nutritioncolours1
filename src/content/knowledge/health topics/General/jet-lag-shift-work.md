# Jet Lag / Shift Work

![Jet Lag / Shift Work](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

## Solution

## Natural Approach

## Scientific Name

Circadian Rhythm Disorder

## Cause

Time zones/night shifts.

## Hidden Fact

The body clock shifts 1 hour per day; flying east is harder.

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

Melatonin, Magnesium.

## Lifestyle

Light therapy (sun AM, blue-blockers PM).
