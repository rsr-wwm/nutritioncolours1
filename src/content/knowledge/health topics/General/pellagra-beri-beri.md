# Pellagra / Beri-Beri

Whole grains, meat, legumes.

## Scientific Name

Niacin (B3) / Thiamine (B1) Deficiency

## Cause

Poor diet or alcoholism.

## Hidden Fact

Alcohol destroys B vitamins, causing nerve paralysis.

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

B-Complex vitamins.

## Lifestyle

Reduce alcohol (destroys B vitamins).
