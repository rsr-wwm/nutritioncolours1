## Scientific Name

Sleep-onset/maintenance Insomnia

## Cause

Caffeine/blue light ruin rhythm.

## Hidden Fact

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

Melatonin, Valerian root, Magnesium Glycinate.

## Lifestyle

Dark room; no screens 1 hr before bed.
