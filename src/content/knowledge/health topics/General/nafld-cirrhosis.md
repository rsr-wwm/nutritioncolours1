# NAFLD Cirrhosis

![NAFLD Cirrhosis](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Late-stage scarring from alcohol/fat.

## Solution

Extreme low-fat, zero alcohol/sugar.

## Natural Approach

- Extreme low-fat, zero alcohol/sugar.

## Scientific Name

Liver Fibrosis

## Cause

Late-stage scarring from alcohol/fat.

## Hidden Fact

The liver can regenerate, but cirrhosis is irreversible.

## When Age

50s+

## Herbs

Milk Thistle, NAC, SAM-e.

## Lifestyle

Absolute alcohol cessation.
