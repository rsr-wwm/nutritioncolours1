# PKU

![PKU](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Genetic lack of enzyme.

## Solution

Strict low-protein medical diet.

## Natural Approach

- Strict low-protein medical diet.

## Scientific Name

Phenylketonuria

## Cause

Genetic lack of enzyme.

## Hidden Fact

Eating protein (meat, dairy) causes severe brain damage.

## When Age

Birth+

## Herbs

Medical amino acid formula.

## Lifestyle

Lifelong diet adherence.
