# Sarcopenia

![Sarcopenia](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Aging + no protein + sitting.

## Solution

High protein (leucine) at every meal.

## Natural Approach

- High protein (leucine) at every meal.

## Scientific Name

Muscle Loss

## Cause

Aging + no protein + sitting.

## Hidden Fact

Muscle tissue is the body's largest reservoir of amino acids.

## When Age

60s+

## Herbs

Creatine, Whey Protein, Vit D.

## Lifestyle

Resistance training 3x/week.
