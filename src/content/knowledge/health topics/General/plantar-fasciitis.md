# Plantar Fasciitis

![Plantar Fasciitis](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Obesity + bad shoes tear fascia.

## Solution

Weight loss diet.

## Natural Approach

- Weight loss diet.

## Scientific Name

Plantar Heel Pain

## Cause

Obesity + bad shoes tear fascia.

## Hidden Fact

The pain is micro-tears in the foot arch ligament.

## When Age

40s+

## Herbs

Turmeric, Bromelain.

## Lifestyle

Orthotic inserts; daily calf stretching.
