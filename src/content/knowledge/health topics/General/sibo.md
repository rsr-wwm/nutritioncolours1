# SIBO

![SIBO](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Low motility lets bacteria climb.

## Solution

Specific Carb Diet (SCD) / Low-FODMAP.

## Natural Approach

- Specific Carb Diet (SCD) / Low-FODMAP.

## Scientific Name

Small Intestine Bacterial Overgrowth

## Cause

Low motility lets bacteria climb.

## Hidden Fact

Antibiotics wipe out good bacteria, letting SIBO explode.

## When Age

30s+

## Herbs

Oregano oil, Berberine, Allicin.

## Lifestyle

Prokinetics (ginger); meal spacing.
