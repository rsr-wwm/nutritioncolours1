## Solution

## Cause

Straining from hard stools.

## Hidden Fact

Passing a stone is often compared to childbirth in pain scale.

## When Age

30s+

## Herbs

Witch Hazel, Rutin, Psyllium.

## Lifestyle

Squatting stool; don't sit >5 mins.
