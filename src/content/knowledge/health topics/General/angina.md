# Angina

![Angina](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Heart arteries narrowed by plaque.

## Solution

Heart-healthy, low-fat diet.

## Natural Approach

- Heart-healthy, low-fat diet.

## Scientific Name

Angina Pectoris

## Cause

Heart arteries narrowed by plaque.

## Hidden Fact

Stable angina stops when you rest; unstable is a heart attack warning.

## When Age

50s+

## Herbs

CoQ10, L-Carnitine, Magnesium.

## Lifestyle

Weight loss; cardio rehab.
