# Tinnitus

![Tinnitus](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

High salt/caffeine alters ear fluid.

## Solution

Low sodium, low caffeine diet.

## Natural Approach

- Low sodium, low caffeine diet.

## Scientific Name

Ringing in Ears

## Cause

High salt/caffeine alters ear fluid.

## Hidden Fact

There is no cure; sound therapy retrains the brain to ignore it.

## When Age

50s+

## Herbs

Ginkgo Biloba, Zinc, B12.

## Lifestyle

White noise machines; stress mgmt.
