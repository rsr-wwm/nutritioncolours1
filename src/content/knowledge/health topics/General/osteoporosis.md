# Osteoporosis

![Osteoporosis](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Lack of Vit D/calcium + no exercise.

## Solution

Dairy, sardines, leafy greens.

## Natural Approach

- Dairy, sardines, leafy greens.

## Scientific Name

Bone Thinning

## Cause

Lack of Vit D/calcium + no exercise.

## Hidden Fact

Bones are living tissue; weight-bearing exercise builds them.

## When Age

50s+

## Herbs

Calcium, Vit D3, Vit K2, Magnesium.

## Lifestyle

Weight-bearing exercise (lifting weights).
