# Celiac Disease

![Celiac Disease](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Gluten triggers immune attack.

## Solution

100% strict gluten-free diet.

## Natural Approach

- 100% strict gluten-free diet.

## Scientific Name

Coeliac Disease

## Cause

Gluten triggers immune attack.

## Hidden Fact

It's an immune reaction, not the same as gluten sensitivity.

## When Age

Infants+

## Herbs

Probiotics, L-Glutamine.

## Lifestyle

Avoid cross-contamination.
