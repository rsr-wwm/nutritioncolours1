# Heatstroke

![Heatstroke](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Extreme heat without water.

## Solution

Hydrate with electrolytes.

## Natural Approach

- Hydrate with electrolytes.

## Scientific Name

Hyperthermia

## Cause

Extreme heat without water.

## Hidden Fact

Sweat stops; the brain's thermostat fails.

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

Electrolytes, Salt tabs.

## Lifestyle

Acclimatize to heat; take shade breaks.
