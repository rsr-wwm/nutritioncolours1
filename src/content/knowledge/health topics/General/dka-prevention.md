# DKA Prevention

![DKA Prevention](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Missing insulin or massive carb binges.

## Solution

Strict carb-counting, hydration.

## Natural Approach

- Strict carb-counting, hydration.

## Scientific Name

Diabetic Ketoacidosis

## Cause

Missing insulin or massive carb binges.

## Hidden Fact

Breath smells like nail polish remover (acetone).

## When Age

T1 Diabetics

## Herbs

Electrolytes, Potassium.

## Lifestyle

Regular insulin; monitor ketones.
