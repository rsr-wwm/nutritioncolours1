# H. pylori Infection

![H. pylori Infection](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Contaminated food/water.

## Solution

Broccoli sprouts (sulforaphane), no sugar.

## Natural Approach

- Broccoli sprouts (sulforaphane), no sugar.

## Scientific Name

Helicobacter pylori

## Cause

Contaminated food/water.

## Hidden Fact

H. pylori survives stomach acid by creating an ammonia cloud.

## When Age

30s+

## Herbs

Mastic gum, Matula tea, Probiotics.

## Lifestyle

Treat with antibiotics if severe; handwashing.
