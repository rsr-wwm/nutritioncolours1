# Low BP

![Low BP](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Severe dehydration and lack of salt.

## Solution

Increase salt intake, hydrate well.

## Natural Approach

- Increase salt intake, hydrate well.

## Scientific Name

Hypotension

## Cause

Severe dehydration and lack of salt.

## Hidden Fact

Can cause fainting when standing up too fast (orthostatic).

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

Licorice root, electrolytes.

## Lifestyle

Stand up slowly; avoid hot baths.
