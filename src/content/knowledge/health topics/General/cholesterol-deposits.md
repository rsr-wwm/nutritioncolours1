# Cholesterol Deposits

![Cholesterol Deposits](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

High blood fat deposits under skin.

## Solution

Strict low-saturated fat, high fiber.

## Natural Approach

- Strict low-saturated fat, high fiber.

## Scientific Name

Xanthelasma

## Cause

High blood fat deposits under skin.

## Hidden Fact

Can indicate a genetic cholesterol disorder (FH).

## When Age

40s+

## Herbs

Plant Sterols, Niacin.

## Lifestyle

Cardio exercise; weight loss.
