# Chilblains

![Chilblains](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Cold damp air causes spasm.

## Solution

Warm foods, ginger (circulation).

## Natural Approach

- Warm foods, ginger (circulation).

## Scientific Name

Pernio

## Cause

Cold damp air causes spasm.

## Hidden Fact

Tiny blood vessels spasm and leak fluid into toes.

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

Ginkgo Biloba, Vitamin B3.

## Lifestyle

Keep hands/feet warm; avoid rapid heating.
