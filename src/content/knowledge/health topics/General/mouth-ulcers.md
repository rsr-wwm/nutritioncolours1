# Mouth Ulcers

![Mouth Ulcers](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Stress, B12/iron deficiency.

## Solution

Soft foods, avoid acidic/spicy.

## Natural Approach

- Soft foods, avoid acidic/spicy.

## Scientific Name

Aphthous Ulcers

## Cause

Stress, B12/iron deficiency.

## Hidden Fact

Canker sores are not cold sores (not contagious).

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

B12, Folate, Iron, Licorice root.

## Lifestyle

Stress management; gentle toothpaste (SLS-free).
