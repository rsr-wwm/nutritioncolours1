# Pre-Osteoporosis

![Pre-Osteoporosis](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Sedentary lifestyle, low calcium.

## Solution

Calcium-rich foods, Vitamin D.

## Natural Approach

- Calcium-rich foods, Vitamin D.

## Scientific Name

Osteopenia

## Cause

Sedentary lifestyle, low calcium.

## Hidden Fact

The precursor to osteoporosis; completely reversible with weights.

## When Age

40s+

## Herbs

Vit K2, Magnesium, Strontium.

## Lifestyle

Weight-bearing exercise; quit smoking.
