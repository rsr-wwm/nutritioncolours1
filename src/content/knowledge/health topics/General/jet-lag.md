# Jet Lag

![Jet Lag](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Time zones ruin sleep/wake.

## Solution

High protein AM, high carb PM to reset.

## Natural Approach

- High protein AM, high carb PM to reset.

## Scientific Name

Circadian Rhythm Disorder

## Cause

Time zones ruin sleep/wake.

## Hidden Fact

The body clock shifts 1 hour per day; flying east is harder.

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

Melatonin, Magnesium.

## Lifestyle

Sunlight exposure in AM; blue-light blockers PM.
