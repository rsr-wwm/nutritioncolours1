# EoE

![EoE](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Severe food allergies.

## Solution

Six-food Elimination Diet.

## Natural Approach

- Six-food Elimination Diet.

## Scientific Name

Eosinophilic Esophagitis

## Cause

Severe food allergies.

## Hidden Fact

White blood cells swarm the esophagus, causing choking.

## When Age

Kids-30s

## Herbs

Fish Oil, Quercetin, Zinc.

## Lifestyle

Allergy testing; chew thoroughly.
