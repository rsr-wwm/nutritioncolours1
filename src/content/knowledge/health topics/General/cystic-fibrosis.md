# Cystic Fibrosis

![Cystic Fibrosis](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Genetic CFTR mutation.

## Solution

High-calorie, high-fat, high-salt diet.

## Natural Approach

- High-calorie, high-fat, high-salt diet.

## Scientific Name

Cystic Fibrosis

## Cause

Genetic CFTR mutation.

## Hidden Fact

Sticky mucus clogs lungs and pancreas; salt tastes very salty.

## When Age

Birth+

## Herbs

Pancreatic enzymes, Fat-soluble vitamins.

## Lifestyle

Chest physiotherapy; exercise.
