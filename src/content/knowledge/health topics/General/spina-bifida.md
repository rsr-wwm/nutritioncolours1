# Spina Bifida

![Spina Bifida](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Lack of folate.

## Solution

Folate-rich greens, fortified foods.

## Natural Approach

- Folate-rich greens, fortified foods.

## Scientific Name

Neural Tube Defects

## Cause

Lack of folate.

## Hidden Fact

Taking folic acid before conception prevents it entirely.

## When Age

Pregnancy

## Herbs

Folic Acid (400mcg) before conception.

## Lifestyle

Pre-conception prenatal vitamins.
