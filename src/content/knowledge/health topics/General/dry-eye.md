# Dry Eye

![Dry Eye](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Screens + lack of Omega-3s.

## Solution

Omega-3 rich fish, chia seeds.

## Natural Approach

- Omega-3 rich fish, chia seeds.

## Scientific Name

Keratoconjunctivitis

## Cause

Screens + lack of Omega-3s.

## Hidden Fact

It's often a lack of oil (meibomian gland), not lack of water.

## When Age

40s+

## Herbs

Fish Oil, Flaxseed Oil, Vitamin A.

## Lifestyle

20-20-20 screen rule; humidifier.
