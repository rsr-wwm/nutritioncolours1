# Migraines

![Migraines](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Fasting/tyramine foods trigger spasms.

## Solution

Regular meals; avoid aged cheese/wine.

## Natural Approach

- Regular meals; avoid aged cheese/wine.

## Scientific Name

Migraine Headache

## Cause

Fasting/tyramine foods trigger spasms.

## Hidden Fact

It is a neurological disease, not just a bad headache.

## When Age

20s-50s

## Herbs

Magnesium, Butterbur, B2 (Riboflavin).

## Lifestyle

Hydration; dark quiet room.
