# Shift Work Disorder

![Shift Work Disorder](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Night shifts ruin circadian rhythm.

## Solution

Eat high protein on shift, fast overnight.

## Natural Approach

- Eat high protein on shift, fast overnight.

## Scientific Name

Shift Work Sleep Disorder

## Cause

Night shifts ruin circadian rhythm.

## Hidden Fact

Light at night suppresses melatonin, ruining sleep quality.

## When Age

20s-50s

## Herbs

Melatonin, Vitamin D (day sleepers).

## Lifestyle

Blackout curtains; white noise.
