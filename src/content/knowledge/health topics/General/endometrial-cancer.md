# Endometrial Cancer

![Endometrial Cancer](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Obesity makes excess estrogen.

## Solution

High fiber (clears estrogen), low sugar.

## Natural Approach

- High fiber (clears estrogen), low sugar.

## Scientific Name

Uterine Cancer

## Cause

Obesity makes excess estrogen.

## Hidden Fact

Fat cells convert androstenedione into estrogen.

## When Age

50s+

## Herbs

DIM (cruciferous extract), Green Tea.

## Lifestyle

Weight loss; limit alcohol.
