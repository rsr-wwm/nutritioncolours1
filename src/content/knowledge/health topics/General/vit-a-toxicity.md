# Vit A Toxicity

![Vit A Toxicity](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Eating excessive animal liver.

## Solution

Stop eating liver/stop supplements.

## Natural Approach

- Stop eating liver/stop supplements.

## Scientific Name

Hypervitaminosis A

## Cause

Eating excessive animal liver.

## Hidden Fact

Polar bear liver is so high in Vit A it is fatal to humans.

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

None (stop the cause).

## Lifestyle

Hydration; symptom management.
