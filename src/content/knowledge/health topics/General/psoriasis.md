![Psoriasis](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Alcohol/smoking trigger overgrowth.

## Solution

Anti-inflammatory, gluten-free (for some).

## Natural Approach

- Anti-inflammatory, gluten-free (for some).

## Scientific Name

Psoriasis Vulgaris

## Cause

Alcohol/smoking trigger overgrowth.

## Hidden Fact

The skin replaces itself in 3 days instead of 28.

## When Age

20s-50s

## Herbs

Oregon Grape, Turmeric, Omega-3.

## Lifestyle

Sunlight exposure; quit smoking/alcohol.
