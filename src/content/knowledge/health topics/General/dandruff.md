# Dandruff

![Dandruff](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Sugar and stress feed yeast.

## Solution

Low-sugar diet, probiotics.

## Natural Approach

- Low-sugar diet, probiotics.

## Scientific Name

Seborrheic Dermatitis

## Cause

Sugar and stress feed yeast.

## Hidden Fact

The yeast (Malassezia) lives on everyone's scalp but overgrows.

## When Age

Teens+

## Herbs

Zinc, Starflower oil, Tea tree oil.

## Lifestyle

Manage stress; anti-dandruff shampoos.
