# Dehydration

![Dehydration](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Replacing water with soda/caffeine.

## Solution

3L water daily, electrolytes.

## Natural Approach

- 3L water daily, electrolytes.

## Scientific Name

Hypohydration

## Cause

Replacing water with soda/caffeine.

## Hidden Fact

Thirst is a late sign; dark urine is an early warning.

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

Electrolyte powders, Coconut water.

## Lifestyle

Carry water bottle; avoid hot sun.
