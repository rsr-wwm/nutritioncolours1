# Gluten Sensitivity

![Gluten Sensitivity](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Eating wheat causes inflammation.

## Solution

Remove gluten/wheat completely.

## Natural Approach

- Remove gluten/wheat completely.

## Scientific Name

Non-Celiac Gluten Sensitivity

## Cause

Eating wheat causes inflammation.

## Hidden Fact

Can develop suddenly to foods eaten safely for years.

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

Probiotics, L-Glutamine.

## Lifestyle

Read labels for hidden wheat.
