# Restless Legs

![Restless Legs](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Iron/magnesium deficiency.

## Solution

Iron-rich foods (red meat, spinach).

## Natural Approach

- Iron-rich foods (red meat, spinach).

## Scientific Name

Restless Legs Syndrome (RLS)

## Cause

Iron/magnesium deficiency.

## Hidden Fact

Symptoms only happen at night or when lying still.

## When Age

40s+

## Herbs

Iron Bisglycinate, Magnesium, Folate.

## Lifestyle

Cut caffeine; leg stretches before bed.
