# High BP

![High BP](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

High salt/stress stiffens arteries.

## Solution

DASH diet (high potassium, low sodium).

## Natural Approach

- DASH diet (high potassium, low sodium).

## Scientific Name

Hypertension

## Cause

High salt/stress stiffens arteries.

## Hidden Fact

Silent killer; often zero symptoms until a stroke.

## When Age

40s+

## Herbs

Garlic, Hibiscus, Magnesium.

## Lifestyle

Stress management; quit smoking.
