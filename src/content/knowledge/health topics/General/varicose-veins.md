# Varicose Veins

![Varicose Veins](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Standing too long; weak vein valves.

## Solution

High fiber (prevents straining).

## Natural Approach

- High fiber (prevents straining).

## Scientific Name

Varicosities

## Cause

Standing too long; weak vein valves.

## Hidden Fact

Everyone has these veins; they only become 'varicose' when swollen.

## When Age

30s+

## Herbs

Horse Chestnut, Butcher's Broom.

## Lifestyle

Calf exercises; leg elevation.
