# Type 2 Diabetes Foot

![Type 2 Diabetes Foot](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

High sugar destroys nerves.

## Solution

Perfect blood sugar control (low carb).

## Natural Approach

- Perfect blood sugar control (low carb).

## Scientific Name

Diabetic Neuropathy/Ulcer

## Cause

High sugar destroys nerves.

## Hidden Fact

Patients step on glass and don't feel it, causing infection.

## When Age

50s+

## Herbs

Alpha-Lipoic Acid, Benfotiamine.

## Lifestyle

Daily foot checks; proper footwear.
