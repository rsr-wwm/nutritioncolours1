# Listeria / Toxo / Trichinosis

![Listeria / Toxo / Trichinosis](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Eating unpasteurized cheese, raw meat, bad cans.

## Solution

Cook meat thoroughly; wash veggies.

## Natural Approach

- Cook meat thoroughly; wash veggies.

## Scientific Name

Listeriosis / Toxoplasmosis / Trichinellosis

## Cause

Eating unpasteurized cheese, raw meat, bad cans.

## Hidden Fact

Toxo can alter brain behavior (often found in cat litter).

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

None (prevention is key).

## Lifestyle

Safe food handling; avoid raw dairy.
