# Dental Erosion

![Dental Erosion](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Carbonated sodas, lemon water.

## Solution

Limit acidic drinks, use straw.

## Natural Approach

- Limit acidic drinks, use straw.

## Scientific Name

Enamel Erosion

## Cause

Carbonated sodas, lemon water.

## Hidden Fact

Acid reflux (GERD) at night silently melts back teeth.

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

Calcium, Fluoride mouthwash.

## Lifestyle

Rinse with water after acid/vomit.
