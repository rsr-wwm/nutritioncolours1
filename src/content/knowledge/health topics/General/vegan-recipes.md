<details>
<summary>How do I meal prep vegan food for the week?</summary>
<p>To meal prep vegan food, batch-cook a base grain (like quinoa or rice), roast a large tray of vegetables, and prepare a plant-based protein (like baked tofu or simmered lentils). Store them separately in airtight containers and mix and match with different vegan sauces throughout the week.</p>
</details>
