# Pre-Eclampsia

![Pre-Eclampsia](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Poor diet/obesity triggers BP.

## Solution

High calcium, magnesium-rich foods.

## Natural Approach

- High calcium, magnesium-rich foods.

## Scientific Name

Toxemia of Pregnancy

## Cause

Poor diet/obesity triggers BP.

## Hidden Fact

Gestational hypertension typically resolves after delivery. Management focuses on monitoring and nutrition.

## When Age

Pregnancy

## Herbs

Baby Aspirin (med), Calcium, Magnesium.

## Lifestyle

BP monitoring; bed rest if severe.
