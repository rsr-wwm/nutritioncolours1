# Metabolic Syndrome

![Metabolic Syndrome](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Junk diet causes simultaneous metabolic failures.

## Solution

Mediterranean diet, whole foods.

## Natural Approach

- Mediterranean diet, whole foods.

## Scientific Name

Syndrome X

## Cause

Junk diet causes simultaneous metabolic failures.

## Hidden Fact

Having 3 of 5 criteria (BP, sugar, belly fat, low HDL, high TG) means you have it.

## When Age

40s+

## Herbs

Omega-3, Magnesium, Berberine.

## Lifestyle

Cardio 150 mins/week; lose belly fat.
