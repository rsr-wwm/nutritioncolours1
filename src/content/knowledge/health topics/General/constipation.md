# Constipation

![Constipation](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Low fiber/dehydration slows colon.

## Solution

Psyllium husk, prunes, greens.

## Natural Approach

- Psyllium husk, prunes, greens.

## Scientific Name

Obstipation

## Cause

Low fiber/dehydration slows colon.

## Hidden Fact

Ignoring the urge stretches the colon, making it lazy.

## When Age

All ages

## Herbs

Magnesium Citrate, Senna.

## Lifestyle

Squatting position; daily walking.
