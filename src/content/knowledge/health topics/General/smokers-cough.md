# Smoker's Cough

![Smoker's Cough](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Smoking permanently destroys lung sacs.

## Solution

High antioxidant foods.

## Natural Approach

- High antioxidant foods.

## Scientific Name

COPD

## Cause

Smoking permanently destroys lung sacs.

## Hidden Fact

Once lung tissue is destroyed, it does not grow back.

## When Age

50s+

## Herbs

NAC, Vitamin C, Magnesium.

## Lifestyle

Quit smoking; pulmonary rehab.
