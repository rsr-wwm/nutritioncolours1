# G6PD Deficiency

![G6PD Deficiency](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Genetic enzyme lack.

## Solution

Strictly avoid fava beans/legumes.

## Natural Approach

- Strictly avoid fava beans/legumes.

## Scientific Name

Favism

## Cause

Genetic enzyme lack.

## Hidden Fact

Eating fava beans causes sudden, severe anemia.

## When Age

Birth+

## Herbs

Folic Acid, Vitamin E.

## Lifestyle

Avoid certain malaria drugs/sulfa drugs.
