# Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver

![Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Excess fructose/sugar.

## Solution

Zero refined sugar, low fructose.

## Natural Approach

- Zero refined sugar, low fructose.

## Scientific Name

NAFLD / MASLD

## Cause

Excess fructose/sugar.

## Hidden Fact

Fructose (sugar) is metabolized directly into liver fat.

## When Age

40s+

## Herbs

Milk Thistle, NAC, Choline.

## Lifestyle

Lose 7-10% body weight; daily walking.
