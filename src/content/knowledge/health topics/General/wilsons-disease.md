# Wilson's Disease

![Wilson's Disease](/images/nutritioncolours-default.svg)

## Problem

Genetic defect prevents copper excretion.

## Solution

Avoid copper foods (shellfish, nuts, choc).

## Natural Approach

- Avoid copper foods (shellfish, nuts, choc).

## Scientific Name

Hepatolenticular Degeneration

## Cause

Genetic defect prevents copper excretion.

## Hidden Fact

Causes golden rings around the eyes (Kayser-Fleischer rings).

## When Age

Childhood-30s

## Herbs

Zinc (blocks copper absorption).

## Lifestyle

Lifelong diet + chelation therapy.
