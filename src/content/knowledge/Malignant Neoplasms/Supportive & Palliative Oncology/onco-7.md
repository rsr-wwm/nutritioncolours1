## Overview

Cancer pain is managed using a stepwise approach, often guided by principles from the World Health Organization's analgesic ladder, which escalates from non-opioid medications to weaker and then stronger opioids, combined as needed with adjuvant medications for specific pain types. Treatment is individualized by the oncology or palliative care team based on pain severity, type, and patient factors.

## Nutritional Considerations

- **Opioid-induced constipation**: This is one of the most common side effects of opioid pain medications. Adequate fluid intake, dietary fiber, and physical activity as tolerated can help, but dietary measures alone are often not enough — most patients also need a scheduled laxative regimen prescribed by their care team, since opioid-induced constipation works through a different mechanism than typical constipation.
- **Nausea**: Can occur, especially when starting or increasing opioid doses; this often improves after the first days of treatment and can be managed with antiemetics if needed.
- **Appetite changes**: Pain itself, as well as some pain medications, can affect appetite. Adequately managing pain often improves a patient's ability and interest in eating.
- **Not stopping medication over side effects**: Patients should be encouraged to report GI side effects to their care team rather than stopping or reducing prescribed pain medication on their own, since undertreated pain has its own serious consequences.
- **Adjuvant medications**: Some adjuvant pain medications (such as certain antidepressants or anticonvulsants used for nerve pain) can also affect appetite or cause dry mouth; these effects can be discussed with the prescribing clinician.

> **Important:** Nutrition strategies described here are supportive and adjunct to a prescribed pain management plan. They do not replace medications or dosing decisions made by your care team. Always consult your treating oncologist or palliative care team about pain medication side effects before changing your diet or medication routine.
