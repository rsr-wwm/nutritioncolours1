## Overview

*Scientific name: Amaranthus*

Amaranth (rajgira) is a gluten-free pseudocereal seed notably rich in iron and manganese, traditionally popped into a snack or eaten during Hindu fasting periods in India.

**Key nutrients:** Iron, Manganese, Calcium, Plant Protein

**Regional context (India):** Popped amaranth (rajgira) widely eaten during religious fasting periods

## What the evidence says

- **[Strong evidence]** A notably iron- and calcium-dense gluten-free pseudocereal relative to true cereal grains.

## Preparation intelligence

Popped amaranth (like popcorn) is a common lighter-preparation snack form; the flour form is used in fasting-friendly flatbreads (rajgira roti).
