## Overview

*Scientific name: Cannabis sativa*

Hulled hemp seeds are a complete plant protein with a favorable near-1:3 omega-6-to-omega-3 fat ratio; they contain no THC and have no psychoactive effect.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g reference scale)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 553 kcal |
| Protein | 31.6 g |
| Fat | 48.8 g |
| Carbohydrate | 8.67 g |
| Magnesium | 700 mg |
| Phosphorus | 1650 mg |
| Iron | 7.95 mg |
| Potassium | 1200 mg |

**Key nutrients:** Complete Plant Protein, Omega-3/Omega-6 Fats, Magnesium, Phosphorus


## What the evidence says

- **[Strong evidence]** A rare complete plant protein source with all nine essential amino acids, relevant for plant-based diets.
- **[Moderate evidence]** Its omega-6-to-omega-3 ratio (roughly 3:1) is more favorable than most seed oils, which tend to be far more omega-6-dominant.

## Preparation intelligence

Hulled hemp seed (hemp "hearts") is the food-grade product sold for eating — distinct from hemp/marijuana flower, and legal and non-intoxicating since it contains negligible THC.
