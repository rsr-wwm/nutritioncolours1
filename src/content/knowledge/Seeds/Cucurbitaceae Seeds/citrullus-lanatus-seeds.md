## Overview

*Scientific name: Citrullus lanatus*

Dried watermelon seed kernels are a dense source of plant protein and magnesium, traditionally roasted and eaten as a snack in India (magaz) rather than discarded.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g reference scale)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 557 kcal |
| Protein | 28.33 g |
| Fat | 47.4 g |
| Carbohydrate | 15.3 g |
| Magnesium | 515 mg |
| Calcium | 54 mg |
| Iron | 7.28 mg |
| Potassium | 648 mg |

**Key nutrients:** Plant Protein, Magnesium, Iron

**Regional context (India):** Sold dried/roasted year-round as "magaz" snack seeds

## What the evidence says

- **[Moderate evidence]** A dense plant protein and magnesium source often overlooked since the seeds are usually discarded rather than eaten with the fruit.

## Preparation intelligence

Watermelon seeds are typically dried and hulled (kernels only) before eating — the whole unhulled seed has a tough, fibrous seed coat that is not palatable raw.
