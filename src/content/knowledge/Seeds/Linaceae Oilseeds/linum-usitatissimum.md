## Overview

*Scientific name: Linum usitatissimum*

Flaxseed (alsi) is the richest common plant source of the omega-3 fat ALA, alongside exceptionally high fiber — but its nutrients are only well absorbed when the seed is ground.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g reference scale)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 534 kcal |
| Omega-3 ALA | 22.8 g |
| Fiber | 27.3 g |
| Magnesium | 392 mg |
| Thiamin | 1.64 mg |
| Potassium | 813 mg |
| Phosphorus | 642 mg |

**Key nutrients:** Omega-3 ALA, Fiber, Lignans, Magnesium


## What the evidence says

- **[Strong evidence]** The richest common plant dietary source of ALA, the essential plant-based omega-3 fatty acid.
- **[Moderate evidence]** Ground flaxseed's soluble fiber and lignans are studied for modest cholesterol and hormone-pathway effects; whole, unground flaxseed provides substantially less benefit since it is not digested.

## Preparation intelligence

Whole flaxseed has a hard seed coat that often passes through digestion intact — grinding (or using milled/ground flaxseed) is necessary to actually absorb its omega-3, fiber, and mineral content.

### 2. Clinical Efficacy & Evidence-Based Applications
Recent clinical observations suggest that dietary elements contained within **Flaxseed** provide supportive benefits for glycemic control and glycemic index management. ### 3. Bioavailability Optimization & Synergy
To maximize the systemic absorption of key compounds in **Flaxseed**, preparation methods should avoid prolonged exposure to high thermal values, which can denature sensitive active enzymes. Pairing **Flaxseed** with high-quality plant lipids (such as organic cold-pressed oils) significantly enhances the bioavailability of fat-soluble vitamins and active phytochemicals.

## Frequently Asked Questions (Speakable & AEO)

### Q1: What are the primary health benefits of integrating Flaxseed into a clinical nutrition protocol?
**A1:** Integrating **Flaxseed** supports metabolic homeostasis, reduces systemic oxidative markers, and aids vascular and cellular defense systems. Its rich nutrient density helps nourish beneficial gut flora, promoting systemic immunological resilience.

### Q2: How should Flaxseed be prepared to maximize its active nutrient retention?
**A2:** To optimize the retention of heat-labile vitamins and active enzymes, consume **Flaxseed** raw or minimally processed (such as light steaming or low-temperature drying). Avoid boiling or deep-frying, which can significantly deplete its nutritional value.

### Q3: Are there any specific contraindications or lifestyle factors to consider?
**A3:** While **Flaxseed** is highly beneficial as a dietary supplement, therapeutic amounts should be coordinated with a clinical nutritionist. Individuals with specific metabolic sensitivities should monitor biomarker baselines during regular integration.
