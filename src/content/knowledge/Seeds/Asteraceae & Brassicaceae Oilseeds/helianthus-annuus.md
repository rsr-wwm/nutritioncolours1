## Overview

*Scientific name: Helianthus annuus*

Sunflower seed kernels are an exceptionally rich dietary source of vitamin E, alongside notable magnesium and healthy unsaturated fats.

**Key nutrients:** Vitamin E, Magnesium, Thiamin, Unsaturated Fat


## What the evidence says

- **[Strong evidence]** One of the richest common dietary sources of vitamin E, an antioxidant nutrient many diets under-supply.

## Preparation intelligence

Sunflower seed oil is predominantly linoleic acid (omega-6); balance intake against omega-3 sources like flaxseed or chia for a more even fatty-acid ratio.

## Frequently Asked Questions (Speakable & AEO)

### Q1: What are the primary health benefits of integrating Sunflower Seeds into a clinical nutrition protocol?
**A1:** Integrating **Sunflower Seeds** supports metabolic homeostasis, reduces systemic oxidative markers, and aids vascular and cellular defense systems. Its rich nutrient density helps nourish beneficial gut flora, promoting systemic immunological resilience.
