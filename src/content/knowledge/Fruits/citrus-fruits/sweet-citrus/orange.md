## Overview

*Scientific name: Citrus × sinensis*

Orange is a citrus fruit (~47 kcal per 100 g) best known as a vitamin C source, with flavonoids and soluble fiber in the whole fruit.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 47 kcal |
| Water | 86.8 g |
| Carbohydrate | 11.8 g |
| Sugars | 9.4 g |
| Fiber | 2.4 g |
| Protein | 0.94 g |
| Fat | 0.12 g |
| Vitamin C | 53.2 mg |
| Folate | 30 µg |
| Potassium | 181 mg |

**Glycemic index:** 43 — Low GI as whole fruit; juice raises glycemic impact.

**Key nutrients:** Vitamin C, Flavonoids (hesperidin), Folate, Soluble fiber

**Seasonality (India):** Winter

## What the evidence says

- **[Moderate evidence]** Whole citrus intake supports vitamin C status and provides flavonoids linked to vascular health.

## Preparation intelligence

Eat the whole fruit rather than juice to keep fiber and blunt the sugar spike.
