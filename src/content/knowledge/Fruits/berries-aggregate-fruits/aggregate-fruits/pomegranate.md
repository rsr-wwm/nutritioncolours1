## Overview

*Scientific name: Punica granatum*

Pomegranate arils provide ~83 kcal per 100 g and are a rich source of polyphenols (punicalagins) studied for cardiovascular and antioxidant effects.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 83 kcal |
| Water | 77.9 g |
| Carbohydrate | 18.7 g |
| Sugars | 13.7 g |
| Fiber | 4 g |
| Protein | 1.67 g |
| Fat | 1.17 g |
| Vitamin C | 10.2 mg |
| Vitamin K | 16.4 µg |
| Potassium | 236 mg |
| Folate | 38 µg |

**Glycemic index:** 53 — Low–medium GI.

**Key nutrients:** Polyphenols (punicalagins), Fiber, Vitamin K, Potassium

**Seasonality (India):** Autumn–winter

## What the evidence says

- **[Limited evidence]** Pomegranate polyphenols have been studied for blood-pressure and antioxidant effects, though evidence is mixed. *(study citation under clinical review)*
