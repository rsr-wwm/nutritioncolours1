## Overview

*Scientific name: Manilkara zapota*

Sapota (chikoo) is a sweet tropical fruit (~83 kcal per 100 g) high in natural sugars and dietary fiber, popular across India.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 83 kcal |
| Water | 78 g |
| Carbohydrate | 19.9 g |
| Sugars | 16.5 g |
| Fiber | 5.3 g |
| Protein | 0.44 g |
| Fat | 1.1 g |
| Vitamin C | 14.7 mg |
| Potassium | 193 mg |

**Glycemic note:** High natural-sugar content; portion awareness advised for blood-sugar management.

**Key nutrients:** Dietary fiber, Natural sugars (energy), Vitamin C

**Seasonality (India):** Winter–spring

## What the evidence says

- **[Limited evidence]** High fiber alongside its sugars; whole-fruit portions should be modest for glycemic control.
