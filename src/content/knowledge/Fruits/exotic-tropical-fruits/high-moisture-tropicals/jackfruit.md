## Overview

*Scientific name: Artocarpus heterophyllus*

Jackfruit is a large tropical fruit (~95 kcal per 100 g ripe) used as a sweet fruit and, when unripe, as a starchy meat substitute in Indian cooking.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 95 kcal |
| Water | 73.5 g |
| Carbohydrate | 23.2 g |
| Sugars | 19.1 g |
| Fiber | 1.5 g |
| Protein | 1.72 g |
| Fat | 0.64 g |
| Vitamin C | 13.7 mg |
| Potassium | 448 mg |

**Glycemic index:** 75 — Ripe jackfruit has a high GI; unripe (raw) jackfruit is starchier and used savoury.

**Key nutrients:** Potassium, Vitamin C, Carbohydrate (energy)

**Seasonality (India):** Summer

## What the evidence says

- **[Limited evidence]** Unripe-jackfruit flour has been studied as a lower-GI staple substitute; early evidence. *(study citation under clinical review)*

## Preparation intelligence

Unripe jackfruit is a popular plant-based meat analogue; it is starchy, not high-protein.
