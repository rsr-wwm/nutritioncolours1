## Overview

*Scientific name: Ananas comosus*

Pineapple is a tropical fruit (~50 kcal per 100 g) providing vitamin C, manganese, and the enzyme bromelain.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 50 kcal |
| Water | 86 g |
| Carbohydrate | 13.1 g |
| Sugars | 9.9 g |
| Fiber | 1.4 g |
| Protein | 0.54 g |
| Fat | 0.12 g |
| Vitamin C | 47.8 mg |
| Manganese | 0.93 mg |

**Glycemic index:** 59 — Medium GI.

**Key nutrients:** Vitamin C, Manganese, Bromelain

**Seasonality (India):** Year-round (peak monsoon)

## What the evidence says

- **[Limited evidence]** Source of vitamin C and manganese; bromelain has anti-inflammatory activity studied mainly at supplemental (non-food) doses. *(study citation under clinical review)*
