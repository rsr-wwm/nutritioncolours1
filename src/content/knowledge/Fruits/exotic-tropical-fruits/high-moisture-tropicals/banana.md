## Overview

*Scientific name: Musa acuminata*

Banana is an energy-dense tropical fruit (~89 kcal per 100 g) notable for potassium, and for resistant starch when less ripe; ripeness raises its glycemic impact.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 89 kcal |
| Water | 74.9 g |
| Carbohydrate | 22.8 g |
| Sugars | 12.2 g |
| Fiber | 2.6 g |
| Protein | 1.09 g |
| Fat | 0.33 g |
| Vitamin C | 8.7 mg |
| Vitamin B6 | 0.37 mg |
| Potassium | 358 mg |
| Magnesium | 27 mg |

**Glycemic index:** 51 — GI rises with ripeness (~42 unripe to ~62 very ripe).

**Key nutrients:** Potassium, Vitamin B6, Resistant starch (unripe), Magnesium

**Seasonality (India):** Year-round

## What the evidence says

- **[Moderate evidence]** A practical dietary potassium source, relevant to blood-pressure-supportive eating patterns.
- **[Limited evidence]** Unripe-banana resistant starch may support gut health and post-meal glucose responses. *(study citation under clinical review)*

## Preparation intelligence

Less-ripe bananas have more resistant starch (prebiotic); very ripe bananas have more free sugars.
