## Overview

*Scientific name: Psidium guajava*

Guava is an exceptionally vitamin-C-dense tropical fruit (~228 mg per 100 g — several times an orange), also high in fiber and relatively low in sugar.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 68 kcal |
| Water | 80.8 g |
| Carbohydrate | 14.3 g |
| Sugars | 8.9 g |
| Fiber | 5.4 g |
| Protein | 2.55 g |
| Fat | 0.95 g |
| Vitamin C | 228.3 mg |
| Folate | 49 µg |
| Potassium | 417 mg |

**Glycemic index:** 12 — Low GI; high fiber blunts glucose response.

**Key nutrients:** Vitamin C, Dietary fiber, Potassium, Folate

**Seasonality (India):** Winter (Oct–Feb)

## What the evidence says

- **[Strong evidence]** Among the richest common dietary sources of vitamin C.
- **[Limited evidence]** High fiber-to-sugar ratio makes it a favorable fruit choice for blood-sugar-aware eating. *(study citation under clinical review)*
