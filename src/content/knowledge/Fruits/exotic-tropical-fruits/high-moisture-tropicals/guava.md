## Overview

*Scientific name: Psidium guajava*

Guava is an exceptionally vitamin-C-dense tropical fruit (~228 mg per 100 g — several times an orange), also high in fiber and relatively low in sugar.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 68 kcal |
| Water | 80.8 g |
| Carbohydrate | 14.3 g |
| Sugars | 8.9 g |
| Fiber | 5.4 g |
| Protein | 2.55 g |
| Fat | 0.95 g |
| Vitamin C | 228.3 mg |
| Folate | 49 µg |
| Potassium | 417 mg |

**Glycemic index:** 12 — Low GI; high fiber blunts glucose response.

**Key nutrients:** Vitamin C, Dietary fiber, Potassium, Folate

**Seasonality (India):** Winter (Oct–Feb)

## What the evidence says

- **[Strong evidence]** Among the richest common dietary sources of vitamin C.
- **[Limited evidence]** High fiber-to-sugar ratio makes it a favorable fruit choice for blood-sugar-aware eating. *(study citation under clinical review)*

### 2. Clinical Efficacy & Evidence-Based Applications
Recent clinical observations suggest that dietary elements contained within **Guava** provide supportive benefits for glycemic control and glycemic index management. In chronic disease management, these properties offer secondary support to baseline therapeutic interventions.

### 3. Bioavailability Optimization & Synergy
To maximize the systemic absorption of key compounds in **Guava**, preparation methods should avoid prolonged exposure to high thermal values, which can denature sensitive active enzymes. Pairing **Guava** with high-quality plant lipids (such as organic cold-pressed oils) significantly enhances the bioavailability of fat-soluble vitamins and active phytochemicals.

## Frequently Asked Questions (Speakable & AEO)

### Q1: What are the primary health benefits of integrating Guava into a clinical nutrition protocol?
**A1:** Integrating **Guava** supports metabolic homeostasis, reduces systemic oxidative markers, and aids vascular and cellular defense systems. Its rich nutrient density helps nourish beneficial gut flora, promoting systemic immunological resilience.

### Q2: How should Guava be prepared to maximize its active nutrient retention?
**A2:** To optimize the retention of heat-labile vitamins and active enzymes, consume **Guava** raw or minimally processed (such as light steaming or low-temperature drying). Avoid boiling or deep-frying, which can significantly deplete its nutritional value.

### Q3: Are there any specific contraindications or lifestyle factors to consider?
**A3:** While **Guava** is highly beneficial as a dietary supplement, therapeutic amounts should be coordinated with a clinical nutritionist. Individuals with specific metabolic sensitivities should monitor biomarker baselines during regular integration.
