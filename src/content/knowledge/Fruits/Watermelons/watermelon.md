## Overview

*Scientific name: Citrullus lanatus*

Watermelon is a hydrating summer fruit (~30 kcal per 100 g, ~91% water) and a notable dietary source of the carotenoid lycopene.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 30 kcal |
| Water | 91.4 g |
| Carbohydrate | 7.6 g |
| Sugars | 6.2 g |
| Fiber | 0.4 g |
| Protein | 0.61 g |
| Fat | 0.15 g |
| Vitamin C | 8.1 mg |
| Lycopene | 4532 µg |
| Potassium | 112 mg |

**Glycemic index:** 72 — High GI but low glycemic load per serving due to low carbohydrate density.

**Key nutrients:** Lycopene, Water (hydration), Vitamin C, Citrulline

**Seasonality (India):** Summer

## What the evidence says

- **[Moderate evidence]** A dietary source of lycopene; despite a high GI, per-serving glycemic load is modest.
