## Overview

*Scientific name: Malus domestica*

Apple is a pome fruit (~52 kcal per 100 g) providing soluble fiber (pectin) and polyphenols, with a low glycemic index.


## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 52 kcal |
| Water | 85.6 g |
| Carbohydrate | 13.8 g |
| Sugars | 10.4 g |
| Fiber | 2.4 g |
| Protein | 0.26 g |
| Fat | 0.17 g |
| Vitamin C | 4.6 mg |
| Potassium | 107 mg |

**Glycemic index:** 36 — Low GI (~36 with skin).

**Key nutrients:** Pectin (soluble fiber), Polyphenols (quercetin), Potassium

**Seasonality (India):** Autumn (hill-grown; stored year-round)

## What the evidence says

- **[Moderate evidence]** Regular whole-apple intake is associated with favorable cardiometabolic markers in cohort studies. *(study citation under clinical review)*

## Preparation intelligence

Most polyphenols and fiber are in the skin — eat unpeeled.
