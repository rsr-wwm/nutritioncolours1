## Overview

Manganese is a cofactor for enzymes involved in bone formation, amino-acid and carbohydrate metabolism, and antioxidant defence (manganese superoxide dismutase, the main antioxidant enzyme inside mitochondria).

## Recommended intake

| Group | Amount per day |
| :--- | ---: |
| Men 19+ | 2.3 mg (AI) |
| Women 19+ | 1.8 mg (AI) |
| Pregnancy | 2.0 mg (AI) |
| Lactation | 2.6 mg (AI) |

**Tolerable Upper Intake Level (UL):** 11 mg/day for adults from all sources combined. (Values are Adequate Intakes, AI, as evidence was insufficient to set an RDA.)

## Food sources

Whole grains (wheat germ, oats, bran), rice, nuts (hazelnuts, almonds, pecans), legumes, leafy vegetables, tea, and many spices including black pepper. It is abundant in plant-based diets.

## Deficiency

Deficiency is very rare in humans.

## Too much

Excess is usually from inhaled industrial dust or contaminated water rather than food, and can cause a Parkinson-like neurological syndrome ('manganism'). People with liver disease clear manganese poorly.
