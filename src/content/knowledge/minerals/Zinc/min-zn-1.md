## Overview

Zinc is a cofactor for over 300 enzymes and is essential for immune function, protein and DNA synthesis, wound healing, taste, and normal growth and development.

## Recommended intake

| Group | Amount per day |
| :--- | ---: |
| Men 19+ | 11 mg |
| Women 19+ | 8 mg |
| Pregnancy | 11 mg |
| Lactation | 12 mg |

**Tolerable Upper Intake Level (UL):** 40 mg/day for adults. Chronic intakes above this can inhibit copper absorption, impair immune function, and lower HDL cholesterol.

## Food sources

Oysters are exceptionally rich. Other sources include meat, poultry, dairy, legumes, nuts, seeds (especially pumpkin), and whole grains. Phytates in legumes and grains reduce absorption, so vegetarians may need up to 50% more.

## Deficiency

Deficiency causes impaired immunity, poor wound healing, hair loss, taste changes, growth retardation in children, and delayed sexual maturation.

## Too much

Excess (usually from supplements or denture creams) causes nausea and, over time, copper deficiency with associated anaemia and neurological problems.
