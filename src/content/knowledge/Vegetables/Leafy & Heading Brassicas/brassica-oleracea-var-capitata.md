## Overview

*Scientific name: Brassica oleracea var. capitata*

Cabbage is a low-calorie cruciferous vegetable (about 25 kcal per 100 g) notable for vitamin K and vitamin C, with a mild, versatile flavor across cuisines.

## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 25 kcal |
| Water | 92.2 g |
| Carbohydrate | 5.8 g |
| Sugars | 3.2 g |
| Fiber | 2.2 g |
| Protein | 0.96 g |
| Fat | 0.1 g |
| Calcium | 40 mg |
| Vitamin C | 40.2 mg |
| Vitamin K | 59.4 µg |

**Glycemic note:** GI not typically assessed — low digestible carbohydrate per serving.

**Key nutrients:** Vitamin K, Vitamin C, Fiber

**Regional context (India):** Winter (Nov–Feb)

## What the evidence says

- **[Moderate evidence]** A meaningful vitamin C and vitamin K source at very low energy density, useful in volume-based, weight-aware meal planning.

## Preparation intelligence

Fermented cabbage (sauerkraut, kimchi-style preparations) adds live cultures alongside the vegetable's native fiber and vitamin C.
