## Overview

*Scientific name: Brassica oleracea var. botrytis*

Cauliflower is a low-calorie, low-carbohydrate cruciferous vegetable (about 25 kcal per 100 g) popular as a versatile substitute for grains such as rice in portion-controlled diets.

## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 25 kcal |
| Water | 92.1 g |
| Carbohydrate | 4.97 g |
| Sugars | 1.91 g |
| Fiber | 1.9 g |
| Protein | 1.92 g |
| Fat | 0.28 g |
| Vitamin C | 67.1 mg |
| Vitamin K | 16 µg |
| Folate | 57 µg |

**Glycemic note:** GI not typically assessed — low digestible carbohydrate per serving.

**Key nutrients:** Vitamin C, Folate, Fiber

**Regional context (India):** Winter (Nov–Feb)

## What the evidence says

- **[Moderate evidence]** A high vitamin C, low-calorie-density vegetable useful in volume-based and lower-carbohydrate meal planning.

## Preparation intelligence

Riced or roasted cauliflower is widely used as a lower-carbohydrate substitute for rice or mashed potato in portion-managed meal plans.
