## Overview

*Scientific name: Vigna radiata*

Mung beans (moong dal) — cooked — provide about 105 kcal per 100 g and are considered one of the easiest-to-digest common dals, widely used in Indian khichdi and as sprouts.

## Nutrition composition (per 100 g, cooked unless noted)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 105 kcal |
| Carbohydrate | 19.15 g |
| Fiber | 8 g |
| Protein | 7.02 g |
| Folate | 159 µg |

**Glycemic index:** 31 — Low GI (~31).

**Key nutrients:** Folate, Plant Protein, Fiber, Magnesium


## What the evidence says

- **[Moderate evidence]** Widely regarded, and clinically observed, as one of the more easily digested common pulses, useful during recovery from digestive upset.
- **[Strong evidence]** A good folate and low-glycemic-index plant protein source.

## Preparation intelligence

Split, hulled mung (moong dal dhuli) is the gentlest on digestion and a common first solid-food dal in Indian households; sprouted whole mung beans increase vitamin C content and are eaten raw or lightly cooked.
