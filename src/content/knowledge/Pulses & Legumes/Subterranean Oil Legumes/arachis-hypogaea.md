## Overview

*Scientific name: Arachis hypogaea*

Peanuts (moongphali) — dry-roasted — provide about 587 kcal per 100 g; botanically a legume (not a tree nut), and a common allergen requiring careful labeling and clinical caution.

## Nutrition composition (per 100 g, cooked unless noted)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 587 kcal |
| Carbohydrate | 12.86 g |
| Protein | 24.35 g |
| Monounsaturated Fat | 26 g |
| Magnesium | 178 mg |


**Key nutrients:** Plant Protein, Monounsaturated Fat, Magnesium, Folate (raw)


## What the evidence says

- **[Moderate evidence]** A dense plant protein and monounsaturated fat source; energy-dense, so portion awareness matters in calorie-controlled diets.
- **[Strong evidence]** One of the most common food allergens, capable of severe (anaphylactic) reactions — always confirm allergy status before recommending peanuts or peanut-derived products clinically.

## Preparation intelligence

Peanuts are botanically a legume that grows underground, not a tree nut — clinically relevant since tree-nut allergy and peanut allergy are distinct (though sometimes co-occurring) immune responses.
