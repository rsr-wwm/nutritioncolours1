## Overview

*Scientific name: Pisum sativum*

Green peas (matar) — cooked — provide about 84 kcal per 100 g, and are eaten fresh as a vegetable-like legume rather than dried, notable for vitamin K and plant protein.

## Nutrition composition (per 100 g, cooked unless noted)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 84 kcal |
| Carbohydrate | 12.6 g |
| Protein | 5.36 g |
| Vitamin K | 26 µg |


**Key nutrients:** Vitamin K, Plant Protein, Fiber, Vitamin C

**Regional context (India):** Winter (Nov–Feb)

## What the evidence says

- **[Moderate evidence]** A notable plant protein source for a fresh vegetable-style legume, alongside a meaningful vitamin K contribution.

## Preparation intelligence

Fresh and frozen green peas retain nutrients comparably; frozen peas are picked and flash-frozen at peak ripeness, often preserving vitamin C better than "fresh" peas that have traveled and aged before cooking.
