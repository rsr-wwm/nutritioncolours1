## Overview

*Scientific name: Hordeum vulgare*

Pearled barley is a cereal grain with one of the lowest glycemic indices of common grains, driven by its unusually high beta-glucan and total fiber content.

## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 352 kcal |
| Water | 10.1 g |
| Carbohydrate | 77.72 g |
| Fiber | 15.6 g |
| Protein | 9.91 g |
| Fat | 1.16 g |
| Calcium | 29 mg |
| Iron | 2.5 mg |

**Glycemic index:** 28 — Low GI (~28) — among the lowest of common cereal grains.

**Key nutrients:** Beta-glucan (soluble fiber), Dietary fiber, Selenium


## What the evidence says

- **[Strong evidence]** Barley's high beta-glucan content is associated with favorable post-meal glucose and cholesterol responses.
- **[Strong evidence]** Contains gluten — not appropriate for celiac disease or wheat/barley/rye sensitivity.
