## Overview

*Scientific name: Avena sativa*

Rolled oats are a whole-grain cereal notable for beta-glucan soluble fiber, studied for cholesterol-lowering and glycemic benefits, at about 389 kcal per 100 g raw.

## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 389 kcal |
| Water | 8.2 g |
| Carbohydrate | 66.27 g |
| Fiber | 10.6 g |
| Protein | 16.89 g |
| Fat | 6.9 g |
| Calcium | 54 mg |
| Iron | 4.72 mg |

**Glycemic index:** 55 — Low–medium GI (~55 for rolled oats); instant/finely milled oats run higher.

**Key nutrients:** Beta-glucan (soluble fiber), Protein, Iron, Manganese


## What the evidence says

- **[Strong evidence]** Oat beta-glucan is well studied for modestly lowering LDL cholesterol when eaten regularly as part of the diet.
- **[Moderate evidence]** The soluble fiber slows gastric emptying, supporting a lower post-meal glucose rise versus refined cereals.

## Preparation intelligence

Overnight soaking or cooking increases digestibility without destroying beta-glucan content.
