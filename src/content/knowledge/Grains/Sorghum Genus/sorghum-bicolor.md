## Overview

*Scientific name: Sorghum bicolor*

Sorghum (jowar) is a major Deccan-plateau staple grain, naturally gluten-free, with a fiber and antioxidant profile comparable to or exceeding wheat.

## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 329 kcal |
| Water | 9.2 g |
| Carbohydrate | 72.6 g |
| Fiber | 6.7 g |
| Protein | 10.4 g |
| Fat | 3.3 g |
| Calcium | 25 mg |
| Iron | 3.4 mg |

**Glycemic index:** 70 — Moderate–high GI for refined jowar flour; whole-grain and fermented (fermented jowar roti) preparations run lower.

**Key nutrients:** Dietary fiber, Antioxidant polyphenols, Iron, Magnesium

**Regional context (India):** Staple in Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana

## What the evidence says

- **[Strong evidence]** A naturally gluten-free whole grain suitable as a wheat substitute for those with celiac disease or gluten sensitivity.
- **[Limited evidence]** Sorghum bran polyphenols show antioxidant activity comparable to some berries in lab assays; clinical dietary evidence is still emerging. *(study citation under clinical review)*
