## Overview

*Scientific name: Oryza sativa*

White long-grain rice is a staple cereal providing about 365 kcal per 100 g raw, mainly as carbohydrate, with a high glycemic index — portion and pairing matter most for blood-sugar impact.

## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 365 kcal |
| Water | 11.6 g |
| Carbohydrate | 79.95 g |
| Sugars | 0.12 g |
| Fiber | 1.3 g |
| Protein | 7.13 g |
| Fat | 0.66 g |
| Calcium | 28 mg |
| Iron | 0.8 mg |

**Glycemic index:** 72 — High GI (~72 for boiled white rice); cooling cooked rice before eating raises resistant starch and lowers glycemic response.

**Key nutrients:** Carbohydrate (energy), Manganese, Thiamin (B1)


## What the evidence says

- **[Strong evidence]** A dense, well-tolerated source of dietary energy; whole-grain/parboiled forms retain more fiber and micronutrients than polished white rice.
- **[Moderate evidence]** Portion control and pairing with protein/fat/fiber blunts the glycemic response in people managing blood sugar. *(study citation under clinical review)*

## Preparation intelligence

Cooking then refrigerating rice for 12+ hours before reheating increases resistant starch (RS3), modestly lowering its glycemic impact.
