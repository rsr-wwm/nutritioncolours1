## Overview

*Scientific name: Eleusine coracana*

Finger millet (ragi) is a South Indian staple millet exceptional for its calcium content — roughly 10x that of most other grains — alongside a moderate glycemic index.

## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 328 kcal |
| Water | 13.1 g |
| Carbohydrate | 72 g |
| Fiber | 3.6 g |
| Protein | 7.3 g |
| Fat | 1.3 g |
| Calcium | 344 mg |
| Iron | 3.9 mg |

**Glycemic note:** Moderate GI; varies notably with processing (whole grain vs. malted flour/porridge raises glycemic response).

**Key nutrients:** Calcium, Dietary fiber, Iron, Polyphenols

**Regional context (India):** Staple in Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh

## What the evidence says

- **[Strong evidence]** Among the richest plant-based dietary calcium sources of any commonly eaten grain.
- **[Limited evidence]** Traditional fermentation/malting of ragi may reduce phytate and improve mineral absorption; evidence is preliminary. *(study citation under clinical review)*

## Preparation intelligence

Traditionally fermented or malted (ragi malt) before cooking, which may improve mineral bioavailability by reducing phytic acid.
