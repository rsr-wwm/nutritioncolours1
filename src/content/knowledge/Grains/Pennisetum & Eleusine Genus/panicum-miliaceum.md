## Overview

*Scientific name: Panicum miliaceum*

Proso millet is a small, gluten-free ancient grain providing about 378 kcal per 100 g raw, with notably high protein and fiber among millets.

## Nutrition composition (per 100 g)

| Nutrient | Amount |
| :--- | ---: |
| Energy | 378 kcal |
| Water | 8.7 g |
| Carbohydrate | 72.85 g |
| Fiber | 8.5 g |
| Protein | 11 g |
| Fat | 4.2 g |
| Calcium | 8 mg |
| Iron | 3 mg |
| Magnesium | 114 mg |
| Phosphorus | 285 mg |

**Glycemic note:** Moderate GI; whole-grain millet porridge has a lower glycemic response than milled millet flour products.

**Key nutrients:** Protein, Dietary fiber, Phosphorus, Magnesium


## What the evidence says

- **[Moderate evidence]** A gluten-free, protein- and fiber-dense grain suitable as a rotational staple alongside rice and wheat.
