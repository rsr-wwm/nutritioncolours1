## Overview

Magnesium is a cofactor for over 300 enzyme systems, including those governing energy production, protein synthesis, muscle and nerve function, blood glucose control, and blood pressure.

## Recommended intake

| Group | Amount per day |
| :--- | ---: |
| Men 19–30 | 400 mg |
| Men 31+ | 420 mg |
| Women 19–30 | 310 mg |
| Women 31+ | 320 mg |

**Upper limit / risk-reduction:** 350 mg/day — this UL applies only to magnesium from **supplements and medications**, not from food. Excess supplemental magnesium causes diarrhoea; food magnesium is safe.

## Food sources

Green leafy vegetables (spinach), legumes, nuts (almonds, cashews), seeds (pumpkin), whole grains, and dark chocolate.

## Deficiency

Deficiency causes muscle cramps, fatigue, abnormal heart rhythms, and can worsen insulin resistance and high blood pressure; it is common with poor diets, alcohol use, and certain medications.

## Too much

Food magnesium poses no risk; high supplemental doses cause diarrhoea and, rarely in kidney disease, dangerous accumulation.
