# PCOD/PCOS Nutrition Guide – Raichur, Karnataka

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Raichur Residents Are at Higher Risk for PCOD/PCOS

Residents of **Raichur, Karnataka** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Raichur residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing PCOD/PCOS effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.

## Circadian Nutrition for Raichur

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Raichur's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage PCOD/PCOS in Raichur?

Locally available Pomegranate and Guava combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support PCOD/PCOS management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for PCOD/PCOS in Karnataka?

For Karnataka residents, replacing *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat* with *Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for PCOD/PCOS management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Raichur that affect PCOD/PCOS?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Raichur that can worsen PCOD/PCOS. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
