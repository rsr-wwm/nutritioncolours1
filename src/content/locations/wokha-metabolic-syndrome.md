# Metabolic Syndrome Nutrition Guide – Wokha, Nagaland

> **Clinical target biomarker:** Visceral Fat Rating, Waist-to-Hip Ratio & Fasting Insulin

## Why Wokha Residents Are at Higher Risk for Metabolic Syndrome

Residents of **Wokha, Nagaland** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Wokha residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Metabolic Syndrome effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.

## Circadian Nutrition for Wokha

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Wokha's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Metabolic Syndrome in Wokha?

Locally available Mango and Banana combined with therapeutic Turmeric can support Metabolic Syndrome management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Metabolic Syndrome in Nagaland?

For Nagaland residents, replacing *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat* with *Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Metabolic Syndrome management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Wokha that affect Metabolic Syndrome?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Wokha that can worsen Metabolic Syndrome. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
