# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Bada bazar, Meghalaya

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Bada bazar Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Bada bazar, Meghalaya** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Bada bazar residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |

| **Local Fruits** | Mango, Papaya, Chiku |
| **Local Vegetables** | Bitter Gourd (Karela), Spinach (Palak), Eggplant (Brinjal), Bottle Gourd (Lauki) |
| **Famous Produce** | Potato, Onion |
| **Therapeutic Spice** | Holy Basil |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Papaya with Holy Basil for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Bada bazar

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Bada bazar's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Bada bazar?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Holy Basil can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Meghalaya?

For Meghalaya residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Bada bazar that affect Obesity?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Bada bazar that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
