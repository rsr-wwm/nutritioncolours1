# Fatty Liver Nutrition Guide – Tirur, Kerala

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Tirur Residents Are at Higher Risk for Fatty Liver

Residents of **Tirur, Kerala** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Tirur residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Fatty Liver effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |
|----------|-------|
| **Local Fruits** | Pomegranate, Jackfruit |
| **Local Vegetables** | Okra (Bhindi), Bitter Gourd (Karela), Eggplant (Brinjal), Bottle Gourd (Lauki), Fenugreek (Methi) |
| **Famous Produce** | Potato, Tomato, Onion |
| **Therapeutic Spice** | Ceylon Cinnamon |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Steam Eggplant (Brinjal) and toss with a pinch of Ceylon Cinnamon and cold-pressed oil during lunch to support hepatic detoxification.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Tirur

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Tirur's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Fatty Liver in Tirur?

Locally available Pomegranate and Jackfruit combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Fatty Liver management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Fatty Liver in Kerala?

For Kerala residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Fatty Liver management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Tirur that affect Fatty Liver?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Tirur that can worsen Fatty Liver. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
