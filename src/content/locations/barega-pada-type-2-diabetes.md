# Type 2 Diabetes Nutrition Guide – Barega pada, Meghalaya

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Barega pada Residents Are at Higher Risk for Type 2 Diabetes

Residents of **Barega pada, Meghalaya** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Barega pada residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Type 2 Diabetes effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Steam Bitter Gourd (Karela) and toss with a pinch of Ceylon Cinnamon and cold-pressed oil during lunch to support hepatic detoxification.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Barega pada

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Barega pada's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Type 2 Diabetes in Barega pada?

Locally available Banana and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Type 2 Diabetes management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Type 2 Diabetes in Meghalaya?

For Meghalaya residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Type 2 Diabetes management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Barega pada that affect Type 2 Diabetes?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Barega pada that can worsen Type 2 Diabetes. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
