# Fatty Liver Nutrition Guide – Pune City, Maharashtra

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Pune City Residents Are at Higher Risk for Fatty Liver

Residents of **Pune City, Maharashtra** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Pune City residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Fatty Liver effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.

## Circadian Nutrition for Pune City

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Pune City's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Fatty Liver in Pune City?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Fatty Liver management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Fatty Liver in Maharashtra?

For Maharashtra residents, replacing *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup* with *Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Fatty Liver management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Pune City that affect Fatty Liver?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Pune City that can worsen Fatty Liver. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
