# Fatty Liver Nutrition Guide – Sekmai, Manipur

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Sekmai Residents Are at Higher Risk for Fatty Liver

Residents of **Sekmai, Manipur** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Sekmai residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Fatty Liver effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |
|----------|-------|
| **Local Fruits** | Papaya, Guava, Chiku, Jamun |
| **Local Vegetables** | Bitter Gourd (Karela), Spinach (Palak), Bottle Gourd (Lauki), Fenugreek (Methi) |
| **Famous Produce** | Tomato, Onion |
| **Therapeutic Spice** | Ceylon Cinnamon |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Papaya lightly dusted with Ceylon Cinnamon in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Sekmai

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Sekmai's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Fatty Liver in Sekmai?

Locally available Papaya and Guava combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Fatty Liver management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Fatty Liver in Manipur?

For Manipur residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Fatty Liver management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Sekmai that affect Fatty Liver?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Sekmai that can worsen Fatty Liver. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
