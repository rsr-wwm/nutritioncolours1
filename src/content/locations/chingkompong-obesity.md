# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Chingkompong, Manipur

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Chingkompong Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Chingkompong, Manipur** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency among Chingkompong residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Chingkompong

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Chingkompong's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Chingkompong?

Locally available Mango and Banana combined with therapeutic Holy Basil can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Manipur?

For Manipur residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Chingkompong that affect Obesity?

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency in Chingkompong that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
