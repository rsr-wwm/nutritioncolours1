# Dyslipidemia Nutrition Guide – Maidjiphema, Nagaland

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Maidjiphema Residents Are at Higher Risk for Dyslipidemia

Residents of **Maidjiphema, Nagaland** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Maidjiphema residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Dyslipidemia effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.

## Circadian Nutrition for Maidjiphema

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Maidjiphema's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Dyslipidemia in Maidjiphema?

Locally available Banana and Papaya combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Dyslipidemia management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Dyslipidemia in Nagaland?

For Nagaland residents, replacing *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat* with *Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Dyslipidemia management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Maidjiphema that affect Dyslipidemia?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Maidjiphema that can worsen Dyslipidemia. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
