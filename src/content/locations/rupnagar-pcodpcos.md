# PCOD/PCOS Nutrition Guide – Rupnagar, Punjab

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Rupnagar Residents Are at Higher Risk for PCOD/PCOS

Residents of **Rupnagar, Punjab** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Rupnagar residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing PCOD/PCOS effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.

## Circadian Nutrition for Rupnagar

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Rupnagar's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage PCOD/PCOS in Rupnagar?

Locally available Mango and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Holy Basil can support PCOD/PCOS management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for PCOD/PCOS in Punjab?

For Punjab residents, replacing *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup* with *Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for PCOD/PCOS management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Rupnagar that affect PCOD/PCOS?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Rupnagar that can worsen PCOD/PCOS. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
