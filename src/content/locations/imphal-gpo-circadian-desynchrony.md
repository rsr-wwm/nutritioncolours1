## Why Imphal G.P.O Residents Are at Higher Risk for Circadian Desynchrony

Residents of **Imphal G.P.O, Manipur** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Imphal G.P.O residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Circadian Desynchrony effectively.

**With** evidence-based alternatives:

## Circadian Nutrition for Imphal G.P.O

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Imphal G.P.O's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike
