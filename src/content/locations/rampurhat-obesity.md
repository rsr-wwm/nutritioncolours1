# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Rampurhat, West Bengal

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Rampurhat Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Rampurhat, West Bengal** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Rampurhat residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Pomegranate with Fenugreek Seeds for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.

## Circadian Nutrition for Rampurhat

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Rampurhat's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Rampurhat?

Locally available Mango and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in West Bengal?

For West Bengal residents, replacing *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup* with *Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Rampurhat that affect Obesity?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Rampurhat that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
