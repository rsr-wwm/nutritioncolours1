# PCOD/PCOS Nutrition Guide – Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Thiruvananthapuram Residents Are at Higher Risk for PCOD/PCOS

Residents of **Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Thiruvananthapuram residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing PCOD/PCOS effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Thiruvananthapuram

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Thiruvananthapuram's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage PCOD/PCOS in Thiruvananthapuram?

Locally available Papaya and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Holy Basil can support PCOD/PCOS management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for PCOD/PCOS in Kerala?

For Kerala residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for PCOD/PCOS management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Thiruvananthapuram that affect PCOD/PCOS?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Thiruvananthapuram that can worsen PCOD/PCOS. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
