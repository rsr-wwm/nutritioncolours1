# PCOD/PCOS Nutrition Guide – Nagaon, Assam

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Nagaon Residents Are at Higher Risk for PCOD/PCOS

Residents of **Nagaon, Assam** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Nagaon residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing PCOD/PCOS effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |
|----------|-------|
| **Local Fruits** | Mango, Banana, Jamun |
| **Local Vegetables** | Bitter Gourd (Karela), Eggplant (Brinjal), Bottle Gourd (Lauki) |
| **Famous Produce** | Banana, Tomato, Onion |
| **Therapeutic Spice** | Ceylon Cinnamon |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Bottle Gourd (Lauki) with Ceylon Cinnamon for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Nagaon

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Nagaon's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage PCOD/PCOS in Nagaon?

Locally available Mango and Banana combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support PCOD/PCOS management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for PCOD/PCOS in Assam?

For Assam residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for PCOD/PCOS management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Nagaon that affect PCOD/PCOS?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Nagaon that can worsen PCOD/PCOS. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
