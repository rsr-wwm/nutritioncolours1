# Hypertension Nutrition Guide – Palai, Kerala

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Palai Residents Are at Higher Risk for Hypertension

Residents of **Palai, Kerala** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Palai residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Hypertension effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |

| **Local Fruits** | Mango, Banana, Guava, Jackfruit |

| **Therapeutic Spice** | Fenugreek Seeds |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Eggplant (Brinjal) lightly dusted with Fenugreek Seeds in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Palai

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Palai's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Hypertension in Palai?

Locally available Mango and Banana combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Hypertension management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Hypertension in Kerala?

For Kerala residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Hypertension management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Palai that affect Hypertension?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Palai that can worsen Hypertension. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
