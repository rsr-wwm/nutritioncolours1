# Hypertension Nutrition Guide – Sirsa, Haryana

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Sirsa Residents Are at Higher Risk for Hypertension

Residents of **Sirsa, Haryana** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Sirsa residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Hypertension effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.

## Circadian Nutrition for Sirsa

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Sirsa's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Hypertension in Sirsa?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Hypertension management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Hypertension in Haryana?

For Haryana residents, replacing *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages* with *Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Hypertension management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Sirsa that affect Hypertension?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Sirsa that can worsen Hypertension. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
