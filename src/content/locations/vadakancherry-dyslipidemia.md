# Dyslipidemia Nutrition Guide – Vadakancherry, Kerala

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Vadakancherry Residents Are at Higher Risk for Dyslipidemia

Residents of **Vadakancherry, Kerala** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Vadakancherry residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Dyslipidemia effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.

## Circadian Nutrition for Vadakancherry

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Vadakancherry's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Dyslipidemia in Vadakancherry?

### What is the best diet plan for Dyslipidemia in Kerala?

For Kerala residents, replacing *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea* with *Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Dyslipidemia management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Vadakancherry that affect Dyslipidemia?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Vadakancherry that can worsen Dyslipidemia. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
