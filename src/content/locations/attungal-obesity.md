# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Attungal, Kerala

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Attungal Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Attungal, Kerala** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Attungal residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Pair fresh Guava juice with Holy Basil during your peak circadian metabolic window (12:00 PM - 2:00 PM) to reduce systemic inflammation.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Attungal

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Attungal's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Attungal?

Locally available Papaya and Guava combined with therapeutic Holy Basil can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Kerala?

For Kerala residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Attungal that affect Obesity?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Attungal that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
