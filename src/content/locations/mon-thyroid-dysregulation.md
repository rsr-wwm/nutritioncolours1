# Thyroid Dysregulation Nutrition Guide – Mon, Nagaland

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Mon Residents Are at Higher Risk for Thyroid Dysregulation

Residents of **Mon, Nagaland** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Mon residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Thyroid Dysregulation effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Mon

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Mon's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Thyroid Dysregulation in Mon?

Locally available Banana and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Thyroid Dysregulation management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Thyroid Dysregulation in Nagaland?

For Nagaland residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Thyroid Dysregulation management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Mon that affect Thyroid Dysregulation?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Mon that can worsen Thyroid Dysregulation. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
