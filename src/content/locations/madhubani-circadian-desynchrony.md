# Circadian Desynchrony Nutrition Guide – Madhubani, Bihar

> **Clinical target biomarker:** Visceral Fat Rating, Waist-to-Hip Ratio & Fasting Insulin

## Why Madhubani Residents Are at Higher Risk for Circadian Desynchrony

Residents of **Madhubani, Bihar** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Madhubani residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Circadian Desynchrony effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Madhubani

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Madhubani's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Circadian Desynchrony in Madhubani?

Locally available Mango and Banana combined with therapeutic Turmeric can support Circadian Desynchrony management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Circadian Desynchrony in Bihar?

For Bihar residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Circadian Desynchrony management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Madhubani that affect Circadian Desynchrony?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Madhubani that can worsen Circadian Desynchrony. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
