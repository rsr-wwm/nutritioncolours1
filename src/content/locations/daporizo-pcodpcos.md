# PCOD/PCOS Nutrition Guide – Daporizo, Arunachal Pradesh

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Daporizo Residents Are at Higher Risk for PCOD/PCOS

Residents of **Daporizo, Arunachal Pradesh** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency among Daporizo residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing PCOD/PCOS effectively.

| Category | Foods |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Steam Mango and toss with a pinch of Ceylon Cinnamon and cold-pressed oil during lunch to support hepatic detoxification.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Daporizo

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Daporizo's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage PCOD/PCOS in Daporizo?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support PCOD/PCOS management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for PCOD/PCOS in Arunachal Pradesh?

For Arunachal Pradesh residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for PCOD/PCOS management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Daporizo that affect PCOD/PCOS?

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency in Daporizo that can worsen PCOD/PCOS. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
