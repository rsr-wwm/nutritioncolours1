# PCOD/PCOS Nutrition Guide – Murtizapur, Maharashtra

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Murtizapur Residents Are at Higher Risk for PCOD/PCOS

Residents of **Murtizapur, Maharashtra** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Murtizapur residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing PCOD/PCOS effectively.

| Category | Foods |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Pair fresh Guava juice with Fenugreek Seeds during your peak circadian metabolic window (12:00 PM - 2:00 PM) to reduce systemic inflammation.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Murtizapur

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Murtizapur's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage PCOD/PCOS in Murtizapur?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support PCOD/PCOS management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for PCOD/PCOS in Maharashtra?

For Maharashtra residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for PCOD/PCOS management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Murtizapur that affect PCOD/PCOS?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Murtizapur that can worsen PCOD/PCOS. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
