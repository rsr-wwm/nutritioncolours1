# PCOD/PCOS Nutrition Guide – Nagpur City, Maharashtra

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Nagpur City Residents Are at Higher Risk for PCOD/PCOS

Residents of **Nagpur City, Maharashtra** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Nagpur City residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing PCOD/PCOS effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |

| **Local Fruits** | Mango, Papaya, Jackfruit |
| **Local Vegetables** | Okra (Bhindi), Bitter Gourd (Karela), Eggplant (Brinjal) |
| **Famous Produce** | Banana, Onion |
| **Therapeutic Spice** | Ceylon Cinnamon |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Bitter Gourd (Karela) with Ceylon Cinnamon for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.

## Circadian Nutrition for Nagpur City

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Nagpur City's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage PCOD/PCOS in Nagpur City?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support PCOD/PCOS management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for PCOD/PCOS in Maharashtra?

For Maharashtra residents, replacing *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat* with *Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for PCOD/PCOS management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Nagpur City that affect PCOD/PCOS?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Nagpur City that can worsen PCOD/PCOS. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
