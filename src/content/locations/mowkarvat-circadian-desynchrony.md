# Circadian Desynchrony Nutrition Guide – Mowkarvat, Meghalaya

> **Clinical target biomarker:** TSH, Free T3, Free T4 & Anti-TPO Antibodies

## Why Mowkarvat Residents Are at Higher Risk for Circadian Desynchrony

Residents of **Mowkarvat, Meghalaya** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency among Mowkarvat residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Circadian Desynchrony effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Mowkarvat

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Mowkarvat's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike
