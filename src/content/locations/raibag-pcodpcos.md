# PCOD/PCOS Nutrition Guide – Raibag, Karnataka

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Raibag Residents Are at Higher Risk for PCOD/PCOS

Residents of **Raibag, Karnataka** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Raibag residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing PCOD/PCOS effectively.

| Category | Foods |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Steam Eggplant (Brinjal) and toss with a pinch of Ceylon Cinnamon and cold-pressed oil during lunch to support hepatic detoxification.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Raibag

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Raibag's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage PCOD/PCOS in Raibag?

Locally available Guava and Jackfruit combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support PCOD/PCOS management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for PCOD/PCOS in Karnataka?

For Karnataka residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for PCOD/PCOS management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Raibag that affect PCOD/PCOS?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Raibag that can worsen PCOD/PCOS. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
