# Insulin Resistance Nutrition Guide – Saiha, Mizoram

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Saiha Residents Are at Higher Risk for Insulin Resistance

Residents of **Saiha, Mizoram** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency among Saiha residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Insulin Resistance effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.

## Circadian Nutrition for Saiha

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Saiha's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Insulin Resistance in Saiha?

Locally available Banana and Papaya combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Insulin Resistance management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Insulin Resistance in Mizoram?

For Mizoram residents, replacing *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup* with *Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Insulin Resistance management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Saiha that affect Insulin Resistance?

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency in Saiha that can worsen Insulin Resistance. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
