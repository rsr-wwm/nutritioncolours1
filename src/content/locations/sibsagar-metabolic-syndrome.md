# Metabolic Syndrome Nutrition Guide – Sibsagar, Assam

> **Clinical target biomarker:** Salivary Cortisol Rhythm (4-Point) & Melatonin Profile

## Why Sibsagar Residents Are at Higher Risk for Metabolic Syndrome

Residents of **Sibsagar, Assam** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Sibsagar residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Metabolic Syndrome effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.

## Circadian Nutrition for Sibsagar

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Sibsagar's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Metabolic Syndrome in Sibsagar?

### What is the best diet plan for Metabolic Syndrome in Assam?

For Assam residents, replacing *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages* with *Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Metabolic Syndrome management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Sibsagar that affect Metabolic Syndrome?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Sibsagar that can worsen Metabolic Syndrome. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
