# Insulin Resistance Nutrition Guide – Guwahati, Assam

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Guwahati Residents Are at Higher Risk for Insulin Resistance

Residents of **Guwahati, Assam** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Guwahati residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Insulin Resistance effectively.

| Category | Foods |

| **Therapeutic Spice** | Ceylon Cinnamon |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Mango with Ceylon Cinnamon for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Guwahati

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Guwahati's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Insulin Resistance in Guwahati?

Locally available Mango and Chiku combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Insulin Resistance management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Insulin Resistance in Assam?

For Assam residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Insulin Resistance management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Guwahati that affect Insulin Resistance?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Guwahati that can worsen Insulin Resistance. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
