# Hypertension Nutrition Guide – Unjha, Gujarat

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ALT, AST, GGT & Liver Ultrasound FibroScan

## Why Unjha Residents Are at Higher Risk for Hypertension

Residents of **Unjha, Gujarat** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Unjha residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Hypertension effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.

## Circadian Nutrition for Unjha

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Unjha's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Hypertension in Unjha?

Locally available Banana and Papaya combined with therapeutic Turmeric can support Hypertension management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Hypertension in Gujarat?

For Gujarat residents, replacing *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages* with *Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Hypertension management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Unjha that affect Hypertension?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Unjha that can worsen Hypertension. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
