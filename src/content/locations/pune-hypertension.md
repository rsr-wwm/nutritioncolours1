# Hypertension Nutrition Guide – Pune, Maharashtra

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ALT, AST, GGT & Liver Ultrasound FibroScan

## Why Pune Residents Are at Higher Risk for Hypertension

Residents of **Pune, Maharashtra** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Pune residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Hypertension effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |
|----------|-------|
| **Local Fruits** | Papaya, Pomegranate, Chiku |
| **Local Vegetables** | Okra (Bhindi), Bitter Gourd (Karela), Spinach (Palak), Bottle Gourd (Lauki) |
| **Famous Produce** | Banana, Tomato |
| **Therapeutic Spice** | Ginger |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Pair fresh Spinach (Palak) juice with Ginger during your peak circadian metabolic window (12:00 PM - 2:00 PM) to reduce systemic inflammation.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.

## Circadian Nutrition for Pune

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Pune's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Hypertension in Pune?

Locally available Papaya and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Ginger can support Hypertension management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Hypertension in Maharashtra?

For Maharashtra residents, replacing *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea* with *Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Hypertension management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Pune that affect Hypertension?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Pune that can worsen Hypertension. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
