# Fatty Liver Nutrition Guide – Phutmouri, Meghalaya

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Phutmouri Residents Are at Higher Risk for Fatty Liver

Residents of **Phutmouri, Meghalaya** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Phutmouri residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Fatty Liver effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.

## Circadian Nutrition for Phutmouri

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Phutmouri's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

For Meghalaya residents, replacing *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup* with *Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Fatty Liver management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Phutmouri that affect Fatty Liver?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Phutmouri that can worsen Fatty Liver. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
