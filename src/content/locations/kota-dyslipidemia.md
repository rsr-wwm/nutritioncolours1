# Dyslipidemia Nutrition Guide – Kota, Rajasthan

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ApoB, Lp(a), Triglycerides & LDL-C Particle Size

## Why Kota Residents Are at Higher Risk for Dyslipidemia

Residents of **Kota, Rajasthan** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Kota residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Dyslipidemia effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Guava with Ashwagandha for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Kota

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Kota's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Dyslipidemia in Kota?

Locally available Banana and Guava combined with therapeutic Ashwagandha can support Dyslipidemia management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Dyslipidemia in Rajasthan?

For Rajasthan residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Dyslipidemia management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Kota that affect Dyslipidemia?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Kota that can worsen Dyslipidemia. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
