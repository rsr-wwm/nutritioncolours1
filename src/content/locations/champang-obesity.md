# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Champang, Nagaland

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Champang Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Champang, Nagaland** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Champang residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |
|----------|-------|
| **Local Fruits** | Jackfruit, Chiku, Jamun |
| **Local Vegetables** | Spinach (Palak), Eggplant (Brinjal), Bottle Gourd (Lauki) |
| **Famous Produce** | Banana, Onion |
| **Therapeutic Spice** | Fenugreek Seeds |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Jamun lightly dusted with Fenugreek Seeds in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Champang

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Champang's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Champang?

Locally available Jackfruit and Chiku combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Nagaland?

For Nagaland residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Champang that affect Obesity?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Champang that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
