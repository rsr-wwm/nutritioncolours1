# Thyroid Dysregulation Nutrition Guide – Ramlun, Mizoram

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Ramlun Residents Are at Higher Risk for Thyroid Dysregulation

Residents of **Ramlun, Mizoram** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Ramlun residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Thyroid Dysregulation effectively.

| Category | Foods |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Guava with Fenugreek Seeds for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.

## Circadian Nutrition for Ramlun

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Ramlun's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Thyroid Dysregulation in Ramlun?

Locally available Guava and Chiku combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Thyroid Dysregulation management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Thyroid Dysregulation in Mizoram?

For Mizoram residents, replacing *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea* with *Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Thyroid Dysregulation management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Ramlun that affect Thyroid Dysregulation?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Ramlun that can worsen Thyroid Dysregulation. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
