# Hypertension Nutrition Guide – Kattappana, Kerala

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Kattappana Residents Are at Higher Risk for Hypertension

Residents of **Kattappana, Kerala** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Kattappana residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Hypertension effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |

| **Local Fruits** | Mango, Jamun |
| **Local Vegetables** | Bitter Gourd (Karela), Spinach (Palak), Bottle Gourd (Lauki) |
| **Famous Produce** | Banana, Tomato |
| **Therapeutic Spice** | Fenugreek Seeds |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Steam Bitter Gourd (Karela) and toss with a pinch of Fenugreek Seeds and cold-pressed oil during lunch to support hepatic detoxification.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Kattappana

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Kattappana's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Hypertension in Kattappana?

Locally available Mango and Jamun combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Hypertension management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Hypertension in Kerala?

For Kerala residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Hypertension management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Kattappana that affect Hypertension?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Kattappana that can worsen Hypertension. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
