# Thyroid Dysregulation Nutrition Guide – Barasat, West Bengal

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Barasat Residents Are at Higher Risk for Thyroid Dysregulation

Residents of **Barasat, West Bengal** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency among Barasat residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Thyroid Dysregulation effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.

## Circadian Nutrition for Barasat

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Barasat's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Thyroid Dysregulation in Barasat?

Locally available Mango and Chiku combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Thyroid Dysregulation management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Thyroid Dysregulation in West Bengal?

For West Bengal residents, replacing *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup* with *Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Thyroid Dysregulation management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Barasat that affect Thyroid Dysregulation?

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency in Barasat that can worsen Thyroid Dysregulation. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
