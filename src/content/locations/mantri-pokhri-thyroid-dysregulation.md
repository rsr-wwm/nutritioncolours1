# Thyroid Dysregulation Nutrition Guide – Mantri Pokhri, Manipur

> **Clinical target biomarker:** TSH, Free T3, Free T4 & Anti-TPO Antibodies

## Why Mantri Pokhri Residents Are at Higher Risk for Thyroid Dysregulation

Residents of **Mantri Pokhri, Manipur** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Mantri Pokhri residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Thyroid Dysregulation effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Bottle Gourd (Lauki) with Ashwagandha for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.

## Circadian Nutrition for Mantri Pokhri

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Mantri Pokhri's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Thyroid Dysregulation in Mantri Pokhri?

Locally available Pomegranate and Guava combined with therapeutic Ashwagandha can support Thyroid Dysregulation management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Thyroid Dysregulation in Manipur?

For Manipur residents, replacing *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea* with *Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Thyroid Dysregulation management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Mantri Pokhri that affect Thyroid Dysregulation?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Mantri Pokhri that can worsen Thyroid Dysregulation. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
