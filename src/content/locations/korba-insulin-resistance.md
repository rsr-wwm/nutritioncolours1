# Insulin Resistance Nutrition Guide – Korba, Madhya Pradesh

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Korba Residents Are at Higher Risk for Insulin Resistance

Residents of **Korba, Madhya Pradesh** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Korba residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Insulin Resistance effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.

## Circadian Nutrition for Korba

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Korba's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Insulin Resistance in Korba?

Locally available Papaya and Guava combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Insulin Resistance management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Insulin Resistance in Madhya Pradesh?

For Madhya Pradesh residents, replacing *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat* with *Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Insulin Resistance management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Korba that affect Insulin Resistance?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Korba that can worsen Insulin Resistance. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
