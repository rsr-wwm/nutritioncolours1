# Circadian Desynchrony Nutrition Guide – Dungarpur, Rajasthan

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Dungarpur Residents Are at Higher Risk for Circadian Desynchrony

Residents of **Dungarpur, Rajasthan** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency among Dungarpur residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Circadian Desynchrony effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.

## Circadian Nutrition for Dungarpur

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Dungarpur's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Circadian Desynchrony in Dungarpur?

Locally available Banana and Papaya combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Circadian Desynchrony management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Circadian Desynchrony in Rajasthan?

For Rajasthan residents, replacing *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea* with *Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Circadian Desynchrony management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Dungarpur that affect Circadian Desynchrony?

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency in Dungarpur that can worsen Circadian Desynchrony. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
