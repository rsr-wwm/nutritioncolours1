# PCOD/PCOS Nutrition Guide – Pani Chowki Bazar, Tripura

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Pani Chowki Bazar Residents Are at Higher Risk for PCOD/PCOS

Residents of **Pani Chowki Bazar, Tripura** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Pani Chowki Bazar residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing PCOD/PCOS effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Bottle Gourd (Lauki) lightly dusted with Holy Basil in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Pani Chowki Bazar

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Pani Chowki Bazar's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage PCOD/PCOS in Pani Chowki Bazar?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Holy Basil can support PCOD/PCOS management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for PCOD/PCOS in Tripura?

For Tripura residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for PCOD/PCOS management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Pani Chowki Bazar that affect PCOD/PCOS?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Pani Chowki Bazar that can worsen PCOD/PCOS. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
