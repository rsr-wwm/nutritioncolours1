# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Rongra, Meghalaya

> **Clinical target biomarker:** Systolic/Diastolic BP & hs-CRP (High-Sensitivity C-Reactive Protein)

## Why Rongra Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Rongra, Meghalaya** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Rongra residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.

## Circadian Nutrition for Rongra

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Rongra's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Rongra?

Locally available Banana and Papaya combined with therapeutic Garlic can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Meghalaya?

For Meghalaya residents, replacing *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup* with *Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Rongra that affect Obesity?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Rongra that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
