# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Sri Rangam, Tamil Nadu

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ALT, AST, GGT & Liver Ultrasound FibroScan

## Why Sri Rangam Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Sri Rangam, Tamil Nadu** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Sri Rangam residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Bottle Gourd (Lauki) lightly dusted with Turmeric in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Sri Rangam

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Sri Rangam's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Sri Rangam?

Locally available Guava and Jamun combined with therapeutic Turmeric can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Tamil Nadu?

For Tamil Nadu residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Sri Rangam that affect Obesity?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Sri Rangam that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
