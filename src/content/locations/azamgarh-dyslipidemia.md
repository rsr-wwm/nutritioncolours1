# Dyslipidemia Nutrition Guide – Azamgarh, Uttar Pradesh

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Azamgarh Residents Are at Higher Risk for Dyslipidemia

Residents of **Azamgarh, Uttar Pradesh** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Azamgarh residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Dyslipidemia effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Azamgarh

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Azamgarh's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Dyslipidemia in Azamgarh?

Locally available Banana and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Dyslipidemia management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Dyslipidemia in Uttar Pradesh?

For Uttar Pradesh residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Dyslipidemia management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Azamgarh that affect Dyslipidemia?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Azamgarh that can worsen Dyslipidemia. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
