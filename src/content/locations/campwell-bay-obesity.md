# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Campwell Bay, Andaman & Nicobar Islands

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ALT, AST, GGT & Liver Ultrasound FibroScan

## Why Campwell Bay Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Campwell Bay, Andaman & Nicobar Islands** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Campwell Bay residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Bitter Gourd (Karela) with Turmeric for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.

## Circadian Nutrition for Campwell Bay

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Campwell Bay's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Campwell Bay?

Locally available Pomegranate and Guava combined with therapeutic Turmeric can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Andaman & Nicobar Islands?

For Andaman & Nicobar Islands residents, replacing *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea* with *Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Campwell Bay that affect Obesity?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Campwell Bay that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
