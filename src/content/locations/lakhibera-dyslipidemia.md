# Dyslipidemia Nutrition Guide – Lakhibera, Mizoram

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Lakhibera Residents Are at Higher Risk for Dyslipidemia

Residents of **Lakhibera, Mizoram** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency among Lakhibera residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Dyslipidemia effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.

## Circadian Nutrition for Lakhibera

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Lakhibera's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Dyslipidemia in Lakhibera?

### What is the best diet plan for Dyslipidemia in Mizoram?

### Which nutrients are deficient in Lakhibera that affect Dyslipidemia?

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency in Lakhibera that can worsen Dyslipidemia. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
