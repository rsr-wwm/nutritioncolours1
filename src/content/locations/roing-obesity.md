# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Roing, Arunachal Pradesh

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Roing Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Roing, Arunachal Pradesh** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Roing residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.

## Circadian Nutrition for Roing

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Roing's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Roing?

Locally available Papaya and Jackfruit combined with therapeutic Holy Basil can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Arunachal Pradesh?

For Arunachal Pradesh residents, replacing *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages* with *Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Roing that affect Obesity?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Roing that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
