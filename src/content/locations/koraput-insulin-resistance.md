# Insulin Resistance Nutrition Guide – Koraput, Orissa

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Koraput Residents Are at Higher Risk for Insulin Resistance

Residents of **Koraput, Orissa** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Koraput residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Insulin Resistance effectively.

> Steam Spinach (Palak) and toss with a pinch of Ceylon Cinnamon and cold-pressed oil during lunch to support hepatic detoxification.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.

## Circadian Nutrition for Koraput

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Koraput's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Insulin Resistance in Koraput?

Locally available Mango and Guava combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Insulin Resistance management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Insulin Resistance in Orissa?

For Orissa residents, replacing *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea* with *Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Insulin Resistance management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Koraput that affect Insulin Resistance?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Koraput that can worsen Insulin Resistance. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
