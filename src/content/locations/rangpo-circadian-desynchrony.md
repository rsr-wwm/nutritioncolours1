# Circadian Desynchrony Nutrition Guide – Rangpo, Sikkim

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Rangpo Residents Are at Higher Risk for Circadian Desynchrony

Residents of **Rangpo, Sikkim** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency among Rangpo residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Circadian Desynchrony effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.

## Circadian Nutrition for Rangpo

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Rangpo's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Circadian Desynchrony in Rangpo?

Locally available Banana and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Circadian Desynchrony management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Circadian Desynchrony in Sikkim?

For Sikkim residents, replacing *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat* with *Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Circadian Desynchrony management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Rangpo that affect Circadian Desynchrony?

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency in Rangpo that can worsen Circadian Desynchrony. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
