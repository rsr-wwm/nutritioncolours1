# Fatty Liver Nutrition Guide – Mangalore, Karnataka

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Mangalore Residents Are at Higher Risk for Fatty Liver

Residents of **Mangalore, Karnataka** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Mangalore residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Fatty Liver effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |
|----------|-------|
| **Local Fruits** | Mango, Guava |
| **Local Vegetables** | Okra (Bhindi), Bitter Gourd (Karela), Eggplant (Brinjal) |
| **Famous Produce** | Banana, Tomato |
| **Therapeutic Spice** | Ceylon Cinnamon |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Eggplant (Brinjal) with Ceylon Cinnamon for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.

## Circadian Nutrition for Mangalore

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Mangalore's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Fatty Liver in Mangalore?

Locally available Mango and Guava combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Fatty Liver management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Fatty Liver in Karnataka?

For Karnataka residents, replacing *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea* with *Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Fatty Liver management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Mangalore that affect Fatty Liver?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Mangalore that can worsen Fatty Liver. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
