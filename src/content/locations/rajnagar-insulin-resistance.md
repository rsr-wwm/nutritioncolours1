# Insulin Resistance Nutrition Guide – Rajnagar, Tripura

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Rajnagar Residents Are at Higher Risk for Insulin Resistance

Residents of **Rajnagar, Tripura** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Rajnagar residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Insulin Resistance effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Rajnagar

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Rajnagar's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Insulin Resistance in Rajnagar?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Insulin Resistance management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Insulin Resistance in Tripura?

For Tripura residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Insulin Resistance management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Rajnagar that affect Insulin Resistance?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Rajnagar that can worsen Insulin Resistance. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
