# Insulin Resistance Nutrition Guide – Rohtak, Haryana

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Rohtak Residents Are at Higher Risk for Insulin Resistance

Residents of **Rohtak, Haryana** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Rohtak residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Insulin Resistance effectively.

| Category | Foods |

| **Therapeutic Spice** | Ceylon Cinnamon |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Pair fresh Okra (Bhindi) juice with Ceylon Cinnamon during your peak circadian metabolic window (12:00 PM - 2:00 PM) to reduce systemic inflammation.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Rohtak

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Rohtak's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Insulin Resistance in Rohtak?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Insulin Resistance management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Insulin Resistance in Haryana?

For Haryana residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Insulin Resistance management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Rohtak that affect Insulin Resistance?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Rohtak that can worsen Insulin Resistance. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
