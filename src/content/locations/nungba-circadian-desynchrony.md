# Circadian Desynchrony Nutrition Guide – Nungba, Manipur

> **Clinical target biomarker:** TSH, Free T3, Free T4 & Anti-TPO Antibodies

## Why Nungba Residents Are at Higher Risk for Circadian Desynchrony

Residents of **Nungba, Manipur** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Nungba residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Circadian Desynchrony effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.

## Circadian Nutrition for Nungba

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Nungba's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Circadian Desynchrony in Nungba?

Locally available Banana and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ashwagandha can support Circadian Desynchrony management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Circadian Desynchrony in Manipur?

For Manipur residents, replacing *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages* with *Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Circadian Desynchrony management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Nungba that affect Circadian Desynchrony?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Nungba that can worsen Circadian Desynchrony. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
