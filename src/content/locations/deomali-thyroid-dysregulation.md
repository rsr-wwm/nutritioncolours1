# Thyroid Dysregulation Nutrition Guide – Deomali, Arunachal Pradesh

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Deomali Residents Are at Higher Risk for Thyroid Dysregulation

Residents of **Deomali, Arunachal Pradesh** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Deomali residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Thyroid Dysregulation effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Okra (Bhindi) lightly dusted with Fenugreek Seeds in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Deomali

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Deomali's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Thyroid Dysregulation in Deomali?

Locally available Mango and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Thyroid Dysregulation management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Thyroid Dysregulation in Arunachal Pradesh?

For Arunachal Pradesh residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Thyroid Dysregulation management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Deomali that affect Thyroid Dysregulation?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Deomali that can worsen Thyroid Dysregulation. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
