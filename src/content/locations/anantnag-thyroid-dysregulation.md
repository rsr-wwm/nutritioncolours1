# Thyroid Dysregulation Nutrition Guide – Anantnag, Jammu & Kashmir

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ALT, AST, GGT & Liver Ultrasound FibroScan

## Why Anantnag Residents Are at Higher Risk for Thyroid Dysregulation

Residents of **Anantnag, Jammu & Kashmir** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Anantnag residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Thyroid Dysregulation effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.

## Circadian Nutrition for Anantnag

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Anantnag's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike
