# Dyslipidemia Nutrition Guide – Phenk, Nagaland

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ApoB, Lp(a), Triglycerides & LDL-C Particle Size

## Why Phenk Residents Are at Higher Risk for Dyslipidemia

Residents of **Phenk, Nagaland** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Phenk residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Dyslipidemia effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.

## Circadian Nutrition for Phenk

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Phenk's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Dyslipidemia in Phenk?

Locally available Pomegranate and Guava combined with therapeutic Ashwagandha can support Dyslipidemia management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Dyslipidemia in Nagaland?

For Nagaland residents, replacing *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages* with *Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Dyslipidemia management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Phenk that affect Dyslipidemia?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Phenk that can worsen Dyslipidemia. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
