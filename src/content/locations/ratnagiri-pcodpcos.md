# PCOD/PCOS Nutrition Guide – Ratnagiri, Maharashtra

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Ratnagiri Residents Are at Higher Risk for PCOD/PCOS

Residents of **Ratnagiri, Maharashtra** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Ratnagiri residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing PCOD/PCOS effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.

## Circadian Nutrition for Ratnagiri

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Ratnagiri's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage PCOD/PCOS in Ratnagiri?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Holy Basil can support PCOD/PCOS management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for PCOD/PCOS in Maharashtra?

For Maharashtra residents, replacing *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup* with *Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for PCOD/PCOS management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Ratnagiri that affect PCOD/PCOS?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Ratnagiri that can worsen PCOD/PCOS. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
