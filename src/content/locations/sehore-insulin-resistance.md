# Insulin Resistance Nutrition Guide – Sehore, Madhya Pradesh

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Sehore Residents Are at Higher Risk for Insulin Resistance

Residents of **Sehore, Madhya Pradesh** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Sehore residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Insulin Resistance effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |
|----------|-------|
| **Local Fruits** | Guava, Jackfruit, Chiku |
| **Local Vegetables** | Bitter Gourd (Karela), Eggplant (Brinjal), Fenugreek (Methi) |
| **Famous Produce** | Banana, Onion |
| **Therapeutic Spice** | Ceylon Cinnamon |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Eggplant (Brinjal) lightly dusted with Ceylon Cinnamon in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Sehore

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Sehore's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Insulin Resistance in Sehore?

Locally available Guava and Jackfruit combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Insulin Resistance management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Insulin Resistance in Madhya Pradesh?

For Madhya Pradesh residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Insulin Resistance management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Sehore that affect Insulin Resistance?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Sehore that can worsen Insulin Resistance. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
