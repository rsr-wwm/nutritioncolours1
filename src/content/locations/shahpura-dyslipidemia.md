# Dyslipidemia Nutrition Guide – Shahpura, Rajasthan

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Shahpura Residents Are at Higher Risk for Dyslipidemia

Residents of **Shahpura, Rajasthan** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency among Shahpura residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Dyslipidemia effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Steam Bitter Gourd (Karela) and toss with a pinch of Ceylon Cinnamon and cold-pressed oil during lunch to support hepatic detoxification.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Shahpura

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Shahpura's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Dyslipidemia in Shahpura?

Locally available Mango and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Dyslipidemia management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Dyslipidemia in Rajasthan?

For Rajasthan residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Dyslipidemia management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Shahpura that affect Dyslipidemia?

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency in Shahpura that can worsen Dyslipidemia. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
