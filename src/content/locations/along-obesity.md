# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Along, Arunachal Pradesh

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ALT, AST, GGT & Liver Ultrasound FibroScan

## Why Along Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Along, Arunachal Pradesh** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Along residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Along

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Along's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Along?

Locally available Banana and Papaya combined with therapeutic Turmeric can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Arunachal Pradesh?

For Arunachal Pradesh residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Along that affect Obesity?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Along that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
