# Dyslipidemia Nutrition Guide – Faridkot, Punjab

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Faridkot Residents Are at Higher Risk for Dyslipidemia

Residents of **Faridkot, Punjab** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Faridkot residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Dyslipidemia effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Faridkot

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Faridkot's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike
