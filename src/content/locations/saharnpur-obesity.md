# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Saharnpur, Uttar Pradesh

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Saharnpur Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Saharnpur, Uttar Pradesh** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Saharnpur residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Saharnpur

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Saharnpur's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Saharnpur?

Locally available Papaya and Jackfruit combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Uttar Pradesh?

For Uttar Pradesh residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Saharnpur that affect Obesity?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Saharnpur that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
