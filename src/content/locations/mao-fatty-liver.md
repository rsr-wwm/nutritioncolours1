# Fatty Liver Nutrition Guide – Mao, Manipur

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Mao Residents Are at Higher Risk for Fatty Liver

Residents of **Mao, Manipur** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Mao residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Fatty Liver effectively.

> Steam Banana and toss with a pinch of Ceylon Cinnamon and cold-pressed oil during lunch to support hepatic detoxification.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.

## Circadian Nutrition for Mao

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Mao's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Fatty Liver in Mao?

Locally available Banana and Guava combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Fatty Liver management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Fatty Liver in Manipur?

For Manipur residents, replacing *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages* with *Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Fatty Liver management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Mao that affect Fatty Liver?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Mao that can worsen Fatty Liver. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
