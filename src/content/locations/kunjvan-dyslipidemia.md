# Dyslipidemia Nutrition Guide – Kunjvan, Tripura

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Kunjvan Residents Are at Higher Risk for Dyslipidemia

Residents of **Kunjvan, Tripura** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Kunjvan residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Dyslipidemia effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Pair fresh Jamun juice with Fenugreek Seeds during your peak circadian metabolic window (12:00 PM - 2:00 PM) to reduce systemic inflammation.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.

## Circadian Nutrition for Kunjvan

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Kunjvan's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Dyslipidemia in Kunjvan?

Locally available Mango and Chiku combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Dyslipidemia management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Dyslipidemia in Tripura?

For Tripura residents, replacing *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages* with *Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Dyslipidemia management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Kunjvan that affect Dyslipidemia?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Kunjvan that can worsen Dyslipidemia. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
