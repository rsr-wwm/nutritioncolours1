# Hypertension Nutrition Guide – Nongpoh, Meghalaya

> **Clinical target biomarker:** Systolic/Diastolic BP & hs-CRP (High-Sensitivity C-Reactive Protein)

## Why Nongpoh Residents Are at Higher Risk for Hypertension

Residents of **Nongpoh, Meghalaya** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency among Nongpoh residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Hypertension effectively.

| Category | Foods |

| **Therapeutic Spice** | Garlic |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Spinach (Palak) with Garlic for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Nongpoh

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Nongpoh's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Hypertension in Nongpoh?

Locally available Pomegranate and Guava combined with therapeutic Garlic can support Hypertension management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Hypertension in Meghalaya?

For Meghalaya residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Hypertension management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Nongpoh that affect Hypertension?

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency in Nongpoh that can worsen Hypertension. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
