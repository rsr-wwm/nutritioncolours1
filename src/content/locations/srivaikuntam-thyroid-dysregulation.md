# Thyroid Dysregulation Nutrition Guide – Srivaikuntam, Tamil Nadu

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Srivaikuntam Residents Are at Higher Risk for Thyroid Dysregulation

Residents of **Srivaikuntam, Tamil Nadu** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Srivaikuntam residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Thyroid Dysregulation effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Srivaikuntam

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Srivaikuntam's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike
