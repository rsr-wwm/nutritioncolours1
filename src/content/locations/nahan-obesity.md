# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Nahan, Himachal Pradesh

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ALT, AST, GGT & Liver Ultrasound FibroScan

## Why Nahan Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Nahan, Himachal Pradesh** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Nahan residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.

## Circadian Nutrition for Nahan

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Nahan's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Nahan?

Locally available Mango and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Ginger can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Himachal Pradesh?

For Himachal Pradesh residents, replacing *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup* with *Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Nahan that affect Obesity?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Nahan that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
