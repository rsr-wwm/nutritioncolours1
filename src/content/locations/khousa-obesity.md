# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Khousa, Arunachal Pradesh

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Khousa Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Khousa, Arunachal Pradesh** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Khousa residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Papaya with Ceylon Cinnamon for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Khousa

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Khousa's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Khousa?

Locally available Papaya and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Arunachal Pradesh?

For Arunachal Pradesh residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Khousa that affect Obesity?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Khousa that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
