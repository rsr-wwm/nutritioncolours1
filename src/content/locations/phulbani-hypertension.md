# Hypertension Nutrition Guide – Phulbani, Orissa

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Phulbani Residents Are at Higher Risk for Hypertension

Residents of **Phulbani, Orissa** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Phulbani residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Hypertension effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |
|----------|-------|
| **Local Fruits** | Guava, Jackfruit |
| **Local Vegetables** | Okra (Bhindi), Bottle Gourd (Lauki), Fenugreek (Methi) |
| **Famous Produce** | Banana, Tomato |
| **Therapeutic Spice** | Fenugreek Seeds |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Bottle Gourd (Lauki) with Fenugreek Seeds for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Phulbani

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Phulbani's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Hypertension in Phulbani?

Locally available Guava and Jackfruit combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Hypertension management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Hypertension in Orissa?

For Orissa residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Hypertension management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Phulbani that affect Hypertension?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Phulbani that can worsen Hypertension. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
