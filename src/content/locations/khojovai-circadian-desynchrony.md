# Circadian Desynchrony Nutrition Guide – Khojovai, Mizoram

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Khojovai Residents Are at Higher Risk for Circadian Desynchrony

Residents of **Khojovai, Mizoram** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Khojovai residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Circadian Desynchrony effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Jamun lightly dusted with Ceylon Cinnamon in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Khojovai

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Khojovai's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Circadian Desynchrony in Khojovai?

Locally available Banana and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Circadian Desynchrony management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Circadian Desynchrony in Mizoram?

For Mizoram residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Circadian Desynchrony management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Khojovai that affect Circadian Desynchrony?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Khojovai that can worsen Circadian Desynchrony. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
