# Thyroid Dysregulation Nutrition Guide – Atur, Tamil Nadu

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ALT, AST, GGT & Liver Ultrasound FibroScan

## Why Atur Residents Are at Higher Risk for Thyroid Dysregulation

Residents of **Atur, Tamil Nadu** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Atur residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Thyroid Dysregulation effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Spinach (Palak) lightly dusted with Ginger in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Atur

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Atur's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Thyroid Dysregulation in Atur?

Locally available Pomegranate and Guava combined with therapeutic Ginger can support Thyroid Dysregulation management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Thyroid Dysregulation in Tamil Nadu?

For Tamil Nadu residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Thyroid Dysregulation management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Atur that affect Thyroid Dysregulation?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Atur that can worsen Thyroid Dysregulation. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
