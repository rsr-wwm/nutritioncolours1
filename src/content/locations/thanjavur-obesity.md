# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Thanjavur Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Thanjavur residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Thanjavur

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Thanjavur's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Thanjavur?

Locally available Mango and Banana combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Tamil Nadu?

For Tamil Nadu residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Thanjavur that affect Obesity?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Thanjavur that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
