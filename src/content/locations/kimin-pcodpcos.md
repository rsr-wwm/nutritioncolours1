# PCOD/PCOS Nutrition Guide – Kimin, Arunachal Pradesh

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Kimin Residents Are at Higher Risk for PCOD/PCOS

Residents of **Kimin, Arunachal Pradesh** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Kimin residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing PCOD/PCOS effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Bottle Gourd (Lauki) with Ceylon Cinnamon for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.

## Circadian Nutrition for Kimin

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Kimin's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage PCOD/PCOS in Kimin?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support PCOD/PCOS management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for PCOD/PCOS in Arunachal Pradesh?

For Arunachal Pradesh residents, replacing *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea* with *Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for PCOD/PCOS management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Kimin that affect PCOD/PCOS?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Kimin that can worsen PCOD/PCOS. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
