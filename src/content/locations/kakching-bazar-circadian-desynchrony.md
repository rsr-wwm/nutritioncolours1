## Why Kakching bazar Residents Are at Higher Risk for Circadian Desynchrony

Residents of **Kakching bazar, Manipur** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Kakching bazar's tropical monsoonal climate climate:
