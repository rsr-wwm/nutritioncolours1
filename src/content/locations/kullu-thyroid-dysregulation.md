# Thyroid Dysregulation Nutrition Guide – Kullu, Himachal Pradesh

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Kullu Residents Are at Higher Risk for Thyroid Dysregulation

Residents of **Kullu, Himachal Pradesh** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Kullu residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Thyroid Dysregulation effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.

## Circadian Nutrition for Kullu

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Kullu's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Thyroid Dysregulation in Kullu?

Locally available Mango and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Holy Basil can support Thyroid Dysregulation management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Thyroid Dysregulation in Himachal Pradesh?

For Himachal Pradesh residents, replacing *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup* with *Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Thyroid Dysregulation management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Kullu that affect Thyroid Dysregulation?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Kullu that can worsen Thyroid Dysregulation. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
