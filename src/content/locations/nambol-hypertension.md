# Hypertension Nutrition Guide – Nambol, Manipur

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Nambol Residents Are at Higher Risk for Hypertension

Residents of **Nambol, Manipur** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Nambol residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Hypertension effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Bottle Gourd (Lauki) with Fenugreek Seeds for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Nambol

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Nambol's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Hypertension in Nambol?

Locally available Pomegranate and Guava combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Hypertension management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Hypertension in Manipur?

For Manipur residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Hypertension management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Nambol that affect Hypertension?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Nambol that can worsen Hypertension. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
