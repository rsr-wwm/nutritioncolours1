# Thyroid Dysregulation Nutrition Guide – Palanpur, Gujarat

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Palanpur Residents Are at Higher Risk for Thyroid Dysregulation

Residents of **Palanpur, Gujarat** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Palanpur residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Thyroid Dysregulation effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Palanpur

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Palanpur's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike
