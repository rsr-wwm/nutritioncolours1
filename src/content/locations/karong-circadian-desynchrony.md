# Circadian Desynchrony Nutrition Guide – Karong, Manipur

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Karong Residents Are at Higher Risk for Circadian Desynchrony

Residents of **Karong, Manipur** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Karong residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Circadian Desynchrony effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Chiku with Ceylon Cinnamon for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.

## Circadian Nutrition for Karong

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Karong's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Circadian Desynchrony in Karong?

Locally available Jackfruit and Chiku combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Circadian Desynchrony management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Circadian Desynchrony in Manipur?

For Manipur residents, replacing *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat* with *Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Circadian Desynchrony management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Karong that affect Circadian Desynchrony?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Karong that can worsen Circadian Desynchrony. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
