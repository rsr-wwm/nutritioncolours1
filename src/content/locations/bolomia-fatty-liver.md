# Fatty Liver Nutrition Guide – Bolomia, Tripura

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Bolomia Residents Are at Higher Risk for Fatty Liver

Residents of **Bolomia, Tripura** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency among Bolomia residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Fatty Liver effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.

## Circadian Nutrition for Bolomia

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Bolomia's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Fatty Liver in Bolomia?

Locally available Mango and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Fatty Liver management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Fatty Liver in Tripura?

For Tripura residents, replacing *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup* with *Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Fatty Liver management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Bolomia that affect Fatty Liver?

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency in Bolomia that can worsen Fatty Liver. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
