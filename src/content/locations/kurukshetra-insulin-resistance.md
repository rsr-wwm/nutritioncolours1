# Insulin Resistance Nutrition Guide – Kurukshetra, Haryana

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Kurukshetra Residents Are at Higher Risk for Insulin Resistance

Residents of **Kurukshetra, Haryana** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Kurukshetra residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Insulin Resistance effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Bitter Gourd (Karela) lightly dusted with Fenugreek Seeds in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Kurukshetra

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Kurukshetra's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Insulin Resistance in Kurukshetra?

Locally available Mango and Banana combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Insulin Resistance management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Insulin Resistance in Haryana?

For Haryana residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Insulin Resistance management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Kurukshetra that affect Insulin Resistance?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Kurukshetra that can worsen Insulin Resistance. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
