# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Gohana, Haryana

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Gohana Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Gohana, Haryana** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Gohana residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

> Steam Eggplant (Brinjal) and toss with a pinch of Fenugreek Seeds and cold-pressed oil during lunch to support hepatic detoxification.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.

## Circadian Nutrition for Gohana

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Gohana's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Gohana?

Locally available Banana and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Haryana?

For Haryana residents, replacing *Wheat parathas, mustard oil, high sodium pickles* with *Sprouted ragi parathas, wood-pressed mustard oil, and low-sodium lacto-fermented vegetable pickles.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Gohana that affect Obesity?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Gohana that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
