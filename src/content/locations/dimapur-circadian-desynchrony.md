# Circadian Desynchrony Nutrition Guide – Dimapur, Nagaland

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Dimapur Residents Are at Higher Risk for Circadian Desynchrony

Residents of **Dimapur, Nagaland** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Dimapur residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Circadian Desynchrony effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.

## Circadian Nutrition for Dimapur

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Dimapur's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Circadian Desynchrony in Dimapur?

Locally available Papaya and Jamun combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Circadian Desynchrony management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Circadian Desynchrony in Nagaland?

For Nagaland residents, replacing *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat* with *Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Circadian Desynchrony management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Dimapur that affect Circadian Desynchrony?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Dimapur that can worsen Circadian Desynchrony. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
