# Fatty Liver Nutrition Guide – Didwana, Rajasthan

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Didwana Residents Are at Higher Risk for Fatty Liver

Residents of **Didwana, Rajasthan** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Didwana residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Fatty Liver effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Fenugreek (Methi) with Fenugreek Seeds for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Didwana

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Didwana's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Fatty Liver in Didwana?

Locally available Banana and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Fenugreek Seeds can support Fatty Liver management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Fatty Liver in Rajasthan?

For Rajasthan residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Fatty Liver management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Didwana that affect Fatty Liver?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Didwana that can worsen Fatty Liver. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
