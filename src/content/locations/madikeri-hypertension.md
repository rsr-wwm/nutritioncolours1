# Hypertension Nutrition Guide – Madikeri, Karnataka

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ALT, AST, GGT & Liver Ultrasound FibroScan

## Why Madikeri Residents Are at Higher Risk for Hypertension

Residents of **Madikeri, Karnataka** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Madikeri residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Hypertension effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |

| **Therapeutic Spice** | Ginger |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Papaya lightly dusted with Ginger in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Madikeri

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Madikeri's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Hypertension in Madikeri?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ginger can support Hypertension management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Hypertension in Karnataka?

For Karnataka residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Hypertension management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Madikeri that affect Hypertension?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Madikeri that can worsen Hypertension. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
