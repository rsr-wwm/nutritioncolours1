# Dyslipidemia Nutrition Guide – Chera bazar, Meghalaya

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Chera bazar Residents Are at Higher Risk for Dyslipidemia

Residents of **Chera bazar, Meghalaya** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency among Chera bazar residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Dyslipidemia effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Jamun lightly dusted with Ceylon Cinnamon in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Chera bazar

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Chera bazar's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Dyslipidemia in Chera bazar?

Locally available Mango and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Dyslipidemia management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Dyslipidemia in Meghalaya?

For Meghalaya residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Dyslipidemia management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Chera bazar that affect Dyslipidemia?

**Iodine (due to low trace mineral profiles in local water/salt)** is a prevalent deficiency in Chera bazar that can worsen Dyslipidemia. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
