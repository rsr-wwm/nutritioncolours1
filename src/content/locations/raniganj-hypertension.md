# Hypertension Nutrition Guide – Raniganj, West Bengal

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ALT, AST, GGT & Liver Ultrasound FibroScan

## Why Raniganj Residents Are at Higher Risk for Hypertension

Residents of **Raniganj, West Bengal** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Raniganj residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Hypertension effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.

## Circadian Nutrition for Raniganj

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Raniganj's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Hypertension in Raniganj?

Locally available Banana and Chiku combined with therapeutic Turmeric can support Hypertension management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Hypertension in West Bengal?

For West Bengal residents, replacing *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages* with *Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Hypertension management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Raniganj that affect Hypertension?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Raniganj that can worsen Hypertension. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
