# Hypertension Nutrition Guide – Kolosib, Mizoram

> **Clinical target biomarker:** Systolic/Diastolic BP & hs-CRP (High-Sensitivity C-Reactive Protein)

## Why Kolosib Residents Are at Higher Risk for Hypertension

Residents of **Kolosib, Mizoram** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Kolosib residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Hypertension effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.

## Circadian Nutrition for Kolosib

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Kolosib's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Hypertension in Kolosib?

Locally available Mango and Banana combined with therapeutic Garlic can support Hypertension management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Hypertension in Mizoram?

For Mizoram residents, replacing *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea* with *Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Hypertension management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Kolosib that affect Hypertension?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Kolosib that can worsen Hypertension. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
