# Obesity Nutrition Guide – Dhule, Maharashtra

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Dhule Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **Dhule, Maharashtra** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency among Dhule residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

## Locally Available Therapeutic Foods

| Category | Foods |

| **Local Fruits** | Mango, Banana, Pomegranate |

| **Therapeutic Spice** | Ceylon Cinnamon |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Okra (Bhindi) lightly dusted with Ceylon Cinnamon in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.

## Circadian Nutrition for Dhule

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Dhule's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in Dhule?

Locally available Mango and Banana combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Maharashtra?

For Maharashtra residents, replacing *Processed wheat bread, seed oils, high fructose corn syrup* with *Sourdough spelt bread, extra-virgin olive oil, and raw organic honey or stevia.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Dhule that affect Obesity?

**Magnesium (due to soil depletion in leafy green crops)** is a prevalent deficiency in Dhule that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
