# Thyroid Dysregulation Nutrition Guide – Malda, West Bengal

> **Clinical target biomarker:** LH/FSH Ratio, Free Testosterone & DHEAS

## Why Malda Residents Are at Higher Risk for Thyroid Dysregulation

Residents of **Malda, West Bengal** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Malda residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Thyroid Dysregulation effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Malda

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Malda's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Thyroid Dysregulation in Malda?

Locally available Pomegranate and Guava combined with therapeutic Holy Basil can support Thyroid Dysregulation management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Thyroid Dysregulation in West Bengal?

For West Bengal residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Thyroid Dysregulation management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Malda that affect Thyroid Dysregulation?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Malda that can worsen Thyroid Dysregulation. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
