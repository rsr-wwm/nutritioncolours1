# Fatty Liver Nutrition Guide – Hanumangarh Junction, Rajasthan

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HOMA-IR Score & Fasting Insulin

## Why Hanumangarh Junction Residents Are at Higher Risk for Fatty Liver

Residents of **Hanumangarh Junction, Rajasthan** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Hanumangarh Junction residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Fatty Liver effectively.

| Category | Foods |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Guava lightly dusted with Ceylon Cinnamon in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.

## Circadian Nutrition for Hanumangarh Junction

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Hanumangarh Junction's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Fatty Liver in Hanumangarh Junction?

Locally available Banana and Papaya combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Fatty Liver management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Fatty Liver in Rajasthan?

For Rajasthan residents, replacing *Millets, white rice, coconut oil, high carbohydrates* with *Barnyard millet, brown unpolished rice, cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil, and high-protein pulse pairings.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Fatty Liver management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Hanumangarh Junction that affect Fatty Liver?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Hanumangarh Junction that can worsen Fatty Liver. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
