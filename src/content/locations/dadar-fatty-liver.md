# Fatty Liver Nutrition Guide – Dadar, Maharashtra

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ALT, AST, GGT & Liver Ultrasound FibroScan

## Why Dadar Residents Are at Higher Risk for Fatty Liver

Residents of **Dadar, Maharashtra** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Dadar residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Fatty Liver effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Processed meats, refined wheat bread, sugary beverages

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Tempeh or organic chicken breast, almond flour bread, and unsweetened lemon-herb water.

## Circadian Nutrition for Dadar

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Dadar's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Dadar that can worsen Fatty Liver. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
