# Circadian Desynchrony Nutrition Guide – Deeg, Rajasthan

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Deeg Residents Are at Higher Risk for Circadian Desynchrony

Residents of **Deeg, Rajasthan** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber*, and a circadian challenge of erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Deeg residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Circadian Desynchrony effectively.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.

## Circadian Nutrition for Deeg

**Your local circadian challenge:** Erratic sleeping patterns from hot summer afternoons disrupting central clock

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Deeg's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Circadian Desynchrony in Deeg?

For Rajasthan residents, replacing *Polished rice, seafood, refined palm oil, low dietary fiber* with *Black Kavuni rice, wild-caught seafood, cold-pressed sesame oil, and fiber-rich local leafy greens.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Circadian Desynchrony management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Deeg that affect Circadian Desynchrony?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Deeg that can worsen Circadian Desynchrony. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
