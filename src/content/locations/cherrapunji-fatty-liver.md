# Fatty Liver Nutrition Guide – Cherrapunji, Meghalaya

> **Clinical target biomarker:** ALT, AST, GGT & Liver Ultrasound FibroScan

## Why Cherrapunji Residents Are at Higher Risk for Fatty Liver

Residents of **Cherrapunji, Meghalaya** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea*, and a circadian challenge of monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion.

High humidity and tropical warmth reduce digestive enzyme secretion speed, requiring warm therapeutic spices to aid transit.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency among Cherrapunji residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Fatty Liver effectively.

| Category | Foods |

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Gently sauté Jamun with Ginger for dinner to calm the nervous system and prevent late-night cortisol spikes.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.

## Circadian Nutrition for Cherrapunji

**Your local circadian challenge:** Monsoon season humidity shifts affecting digestive transit speed and enzyme secretion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Cherrapunji's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Fatty Liver in Cherrapunji?

Locally available Pomegranate and Jamun combined with therapeutic Ginger can support Fatty Liver management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Fatty Liver in Meghalaya?

For Meghalaya residents, replacing *Refined wheat rotis, processed vegetable oils, high sugar tea* with *Khapli (emmer) wheat rotis, cold-pressed mustard oil, and stevia/cardamom organic tea.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Fatty Liver management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Cherrapunji that affect Fatty Liver?

**Vitamin B12 (due to vegetarian/vegan staple gaps)** is a prevalent deficiency in Cherrapunji that can worsen Fatty Liver. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
