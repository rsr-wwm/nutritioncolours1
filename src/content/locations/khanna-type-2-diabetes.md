# Type 2 Diabetes Nutrition Guide – Khanna, Punjab

> **Clinical target biomarker:** HbA1c, Fasting Blood Glucose & Fasting Insulin

## Why Khanna Residents Are at Higher Risk for Type 2 Diabetes

Residents of **Khanna, Punjab** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat*, and a circadian challenge of late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion.

Constant tropical heat increases fluid and electrolyte turnover, requiring trace mineral replenishment to sustain cellular pumps.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency among Khanna residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Type 2 Diabetes effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Pair fresh Chiku juice with Ceylon Cinnamon during your peak circadian metabolic window (12:00 PM - 2:00 PM) to reduce systemic inflammation.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.

## Circadian Nutrition for Khanna

**Your local circadian challenge:** Late-night heavy dinner culture delaying melatonin release and slowing digestion

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in Khanna's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Type 2 Diabetes in Khanna?

Locally available Banana and Pomegranate combined with therapeutic Ceylon Cinnamon can support Type 2 Diabetes management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Type 2 Diabetes in Punjab?

For Punjab residents, replacing *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat* with *Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Type 2 Diabetes management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in Khanna that affect Type 2 Diabetes?

**Selenium (due to dry soil conditions affecting grains)** is a prevalent deficiency in Khanna that can worsen Type 2 Diabetes. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
