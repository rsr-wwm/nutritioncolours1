# Obesity Nutrition Guide – A.R.T.C, Nagaland

> **Clinical target biomarker:** Visceral Fat Rating, Waist-to-Hip Ratio & Fasting Insulin

## Why A.R.T.C Residents Are at Higher Risk for Obesity

Residents of **A.R.T.C, Nagaland** face a unique metabolic challenge shaped by the local tropical monsoonal climate, wet summers, mild winters, dietary staples such as *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat*, and a circadian challenge of high carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness.

Monsoon weather patterns trigger erratic atmospheric pressure changes, affecting gut motility and joint fluid dynamics.

## Key Nutritional Deficiency to Address

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency among A.R.T.C residents. Addressing this gap is a critical first step in managing Obesity effectively.

### Local Produce Synergy Tip

> Consume raw Papaya lightly dusted with Turmeric in the morning to optimize insulin sensitivity.

## Recommended Dietary Shifts

**Replace** your current staples:
- ❌ Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat

**With** evidence-based alternatives:
- ✅ Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.

## Circadian Nutrition for A.R.T.C

**Your local circadian challenge:** High carbohydrate breakfasts causing mid-day insulin peaks and sleepiness

Aligning meal timing with your body's natural clock is especially important in A.R.T.C's tropical monsoonal climate climate:

- 🌅 Eat your **largest meal between 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM** (peak metabolic window)
- 🌙 Avoid heavy meals after **7:00 PM**
- 🥚 Front-load protein and fibre at breakfast to prevent the mid-day insulin spike

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What foods help manage Obesity in A.R.T.C?

Locally available Papaya and Jackfruit combined with therapeutic Turmeric can support Obesity management through their synergistic phytonutrient profiles.

### What is the best diet plan for Obesity in Nagaland?

For Nagaland residents, replacing *Polished white rice, refined wheat flour, high dairy fat* with *Foxtail millet rice, sprouted emmer wheat flatbreads, and organic A2 cow ghee in moderation.* is the primary dietary shift recommended for Obesity management.

### Which nutrients are deficient in A.R.T.C that affect Obesity?

**Vitamin D3 (due to low sunlight synthesis or indoor shift hours)** is a prevalent deficiency in A.R.T.C that can worsen Obesity. Targeted supplementation and local food choices can help address this gap.
